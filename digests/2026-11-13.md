# AI Daily Digest — 2026-11-13

A quiet day for new same-day drops, so today's entries catch up on a restricted cybersecurity model OpenAI shipped earlier this week and the latest on Anthropic's IPO timeline.

## Model Releases
- **OpenAI releases GPT-6 Cyber, a restricted model for authorized vulnerability research** — Announced November 5 on OpenAI's own site (and reported by The Decoder, TechRadar, Cybermagazine, and others), GPT-6 Cyber is a specialized variant of GPT-6 Astra built for authorized exploit development and vulnerability discovery, initially available only to approved Daybreak Red customers via the API and Codex. OpenAI rates it "Critical" for cybersecurity risk under its Preparedness Framework (up from "High" for GPT-5.6 Cyber) and reports a 48.5% score on the ExploitGym benchmark versus 32.1% for its predecessor, plus use in finding 81 previously unknown high-severity Firefox vulnerabilities. Benchmark and safety figures are OpenAI's own and haven't been independently verified. [Source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-cyber/)

## Other News
- **Anthropic's IPO moves toward a specific pricing date: November 18, trading November 19** — Multiple outlets (CNBC, Nasdaq.com, and others) now report, citing people familiar with the deal, that Anthropic plans to price its Nasdaq listing (ticker "ANT") on November 18 and begin trading November 19 — a firmer timeline than the roadshow-stage reporting covered here November 10–12. The offering terms remain 825 million Class A shares at an expected $70–$78 each; the company hasn't confirmed either date publicly, and valuation estimates among outlets still range roughly $1.2–1.4T. [Source](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/6-things-know-about-anthropics-upcoming-ipo)
