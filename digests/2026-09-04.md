# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-04

## Model Releases
- **OpenAI rolls out GPT-6 Astra, its first "Critical" cyber-tier model** — After teasing the Astra safety report last week, OpenAI began shipping GPT-6 Astra to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users, plus the API and AWS, starting with participants in its application-based cybersecurity access program. OpenAI says Astra can find unknown vulnerabilities and build novel exploits against hardened targets without step-by-step human guidance, and that it refuses 91.5% of cyber-jailbreak attempts in testing, up from 59% for GPT-5.6 Sol. [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/03/open-ai-astra-gpt-6-cyber.html)

## Open Source
- **Institute of Foundation Models releases K2 Horizon, a fully open six-model fleet from 0.9B to 375B params** — Weights, code, training data, and training methodology are all public, which IFM is calling the largest fully open-source model launch to date. The lineup spans on-device sizes (0.9B–7B) through a 375B-A23B enterprise model, sharing a common architecture and tooling, with the smallest models posting state-of-the-art results for their size class on reasoning and coding benchmarks. [Source](https://ifm.ai/k2/press-release/)

## Other News
- **OpenAI and Anthropic head toward a token-price war ahead of dueling IPOs** — With OpenAI targeting a public listing as soon as this month at up to a $1T valuation and Anthropic having confidentially filed for its own US IPO, OpenAI is reportedly weighing steep API price cuts to blunt Anthropic's enterprise momentum (Claude Code helped push Anthropic's run rate from $9B to $47B annualized in five months). Both companies are projected to spend close to $65B on compute in 2026, raising questions about how sustainable a pricing fight would be pre-IPO. [Source](https://www.pymnts.com/artificial-intelligence-2/2026/openai-weighs-price-cuts-as-competition-with-anthropic-takes-shape/)
