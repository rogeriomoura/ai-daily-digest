# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-14

## Model Releases
- **Meta launches Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, its first frontier-class models** — The models mark Meta's re-entry at the frontier since Alexandr Wang's restructuring of its AI division, with a 1M-token context window, multimodal (text/image/video/audio) input, and headline features "Active Memory" (carries context across conversations) and "Active Focus" (sustains multi-step tasks and recovers from errors). Wonder is priced at $4/$20 per million input/output tokens via Meta's API; the more capable Wonder Full is gated to Meta's Muse Code subscription ($50–$200/month). On the independent Artificial Analysis index it scores just behind Claude Opus 5.5 (56 and 52 vs. 58); Meta's own benchmarks claim a coding lead over Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra, which are vendor-reported and not yet independently confirmed. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/12/meta-releases-its-first-frontier-model-muse-wonder)

## Open Source
- **debpalash/VoiceStudio tops GitHub Trending as a fully local ElevenLabs alternative** — The AGPL-3.0 Python project bundles voice cloning, voice design, video dubbing, dictation, transcription, and audiobook generation, running entirely on local hardware with no accounts, API keys, or cloud calls, and claims support for 646 languages. It gained roughly 3,500 stars in a single day, on its way to nearly 65,000 total. [Source](https://github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio)

## Other News
- **Manus parent Butterfly Effect raises $500M+ in its first round since the Meta deal unwound** — The Chinese AI-agent company closed the round co-led by Boyu Capital and IDG Capital, with existing backers Tencent, HSG (formerly Sequoia China), and ZhenFund also participating. It's the company's first raise since Beijing ordered Meta to unwind its roughly $2B acquisition of Manus in April; Manus resumed independent operations in September, and reports put the valuation target around $4B, though the company hasn't confirmed it. [Source](https://equalocean.com/news/2026101022230-manus-parent-butterfly-effect-raises-more-than-500-million-after-meta-deal)
