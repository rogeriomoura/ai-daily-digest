# AI Daily Digest — 2026-08-23

## Model Releases
- **Google ships Gemini 3.7 Flash** — Released three weeks after 3.6 Flash, Google's new "workhorse" model targets coding and agentic tasks, posting notable gains on FrontierCode and DeepSWE long-horizon benchmarks while cutting introductory pricing roughly in half (to $0.75/M input, $3.75/M output tokens). [Source](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-3-7-flash/)
- **Alibaba releases Qwen3.8-Max** — A 2.4T-parameter update to Alibaba's flagship Qwen line, continuing the rapid release cadence from Chinese labs this month alongside Z.AI's GLM-5.3 and DeepSeek's V4-Pro-0813 agent upgrade. [Source](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news)

## Open Source
- **OpenClaw becomes the breakout open-source project of the year** — A self-hosted, open-source personal AI assistant that plugs into WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord and 50+ other platforms, OpenClaw has crossed 15,000+ GitHub stars and 200+ contributors under its new nonprofit foundation, positioning itself as a private, self-hosted alternative to cloud assistants. [Source](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw)

## Other News
- **Anthropic says Claude designed working protein binders in a wet-lab test** — In an experiment run with Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience, Claude (Opus 4.8 and a research preview) generated de novo protein binders for 14 of 15 biological targets, with lab-validated hit rates of 22–35% versus a typical industry rate of 10–15% — including a 40% hit rate on one target where a human design competition managed 3.7%. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/research/Claude-accelerates-protein-design)
- **Cloudflare launches Kitesurf, a browser built specifically for AI agents** — Rather than adapting Chromium, Cloudflare built Kitesurf from scratch on V8 isolates within Cloudflare Workers, using 3–7x less CPU/memory than Chromium for agentic browsing while passing 235,000+ web platform tests and integrating with Playwright, Puppeteer, and MCP clients. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/07/cloudflare-launches-kitesurf-a-browser-built-for-ai-agents/)
- **White House commits over $5B to embed AI in federal science** — The administration is directing more than $5 billion across 15+ federal agencies to integrate AI into national scientific research, alongside a separate White House/HHS sprint to develop consensus benchmarks for evaluating clinical AI tools. [Source](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02332-8)
