# AI Daily Digest — 2026-11-04

Another quiet stretch for new model and open-source releases, but Anthropic's IPO moved from paperwork to a real listing track this week: the company publicly filed its S-1 and disclosed Q3 revenue figures ahead of a planned mid-November Nasdaq debut.

## Other News
- **Anthropic publicly files S-1, targets Nasdaq listing under ticker "ANT"** — Filed October 26, this converts the confidential draft registration (whose $80.7B H1 net loss was covered in this digest on October 30) into a public Form S-1 for an IPO of Class A common stock. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley lead a syndicate of more than twenty underwriters; the filing leaves the share count and price range blank. Separately, people who attended a closed pre-IPO investor meeting on October 14 told Reuters and the Financial Times that Q3 revenue hit $16.8B, up about 45% quarter-over-quarter from $11.6B in Q2 — a slowdown from Q2's roughly 147% sequential jump — with Anthropic also claiming adjusted-basis profitability. Press reports point to a listing the week of November 16, though no date is confirmed and terms remain unfinalized.
