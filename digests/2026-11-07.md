# AI Daily Digest — 2026-11-07

Still no clearly-sourced new model release today, but money kept moving: OpenAI's latest funding round and Anthropic's IPO valuation both got more concrete numbers this week, and a small on-device speech model drew attention in the open-source world.

## Open Source
- **Underdog demos "Woof," a 32MB speech model running fully offline in a browser** — Reported November 6 by TechCrunch, on-device AI startup Underdog showed off Woof, a roughly 31-million-parameter (~32MB) speech model, via a retro browser game where typed text is spoken aloud with no server round-trip. The weights are open for developers to pull and run locally; it follows Underdog's earlier open release of Bark, a ~163MB speech-recognition model built on NVIDIA's Parakeet. Size and accuracy claims are the company's own and haven't been independently verified. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/underdog-shows-off-a-32mb-speech-model-in-new-demo/)

## Other News
- **OpenAI reportedly secures $11B of a target $30B round at a $1.25T valuation** — Per Bloomberg, reported November 6 by TechCrunch, OpenAI has raised about $11 billion in commitments so far toward a round it's hoping will reach roughly $30 billion, with the deal valuing the company around $1.25 trillion — below the $1.4 trillion figure floated a month earlier. Some large investors are reportedly showing only tepid interest, and the round has not yet closed. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/openai-reportedly-raises-an-additional-11-billion-at-a-1-25-trillion-valuation/)
- **Anthropic IPO: investors reportedly push valuation down to $1.5–1.6T, from ~$2T talked about earlier** — Building on this week's broader AI-stock selloff (covered in yesterday's digest), the Wall Street Journal reports institutional IPO buyers have told Anthropic and its advisers they want a valuation closer to $1.5–1.6 trillion rather than the ~$2 trillion previously discussed, citing the tech-stock rout and a Q3 revenue growth slowdown (45% QoQ versus Q2's ~147%). Pricing and share count remain undecided; some investors still think the stock could jump toward $2T by the end of its first trading day if it debuts lower. [Source](https://www.renaissancecapital.com/IPO-Center/Winners-Losers/1570)
