# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-05

## Model Releases
- **Kuaishou launches Kling 4.0, its fourth-generation video model** — The full version follows the Kling 4.0 Flash early-access tier and doubles the single-generation length to 30 seconds (from 15 in Kling 3.0), adds up to 10 in-between keyframes (versus just start/end frames before), and renders lip-synced dialogue in stereo audio across 9+ languages at up to 4K. The developer API isn't live yet, so pricing and request limits aren't public. [Source](https://unifically.com/models/kling-4.0)

## Other News
- **California's "No Robo Bosses Act" becomes law** — Governor Gavin Newsom signed SB 947 on September 30, reversing his veto of an earlier version last October. The first-in-the-nation law bars employers from relying solely on an AI system to fire or discipline workers — a human reviewer must corroborate any such decision using other evidence — and takes effect July 1, 2027; Newsom signed a dozen related AI bills the same day covering workplace surveillance, deepfakes, medical AI, and AI use by lawyers. [Source](https://www.techradar.com/pro/california-governor-gavin-newsom-signs-no-robo-bosses-act-so-an-ai-can-no-longer-fire-you-on-its-own)
- **Nvidia discloses its contracted value with Anthropic now exceeds $180B** — The figure, disclosed September 29, aggregates cloud-compute agreements, equity commitments, and IPO-related talks across multiple deals and intermediaries (including a $35B Lambda cloud deal and a $45B Nscale agreement for Nvidia-powered data-center capacity) rather than a single contract, underscoring how tightly Nvidia's business is now tied to Anthropic's growth ahead of a widely expected IPO. [Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/nvidia-anthropic-contracted-value-180b/)
