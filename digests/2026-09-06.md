# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-06

## Model Releases
- **Meta ships Muse Spark 1.3, claims parity with OpenAI and Anthropic's latest** — Rolling out this week in Muse Code, the Meta Model API, and across Instagram/Facebook AI, the update targets longer-horizon agentic and coding work, using roughly 20% fewer tool calls and 25% fewer tokens than Muse Spark 1.2 per Meta's own comparisons. Pricing holds at $1.25/$4.25 per million input/output tokens with a 1M-token context window. [Source](https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-spark-1-3)
- **Alibaba updates its flagship with Qwen3.8-Max-0902** — A dated snapshot of Qwen3.8-Max further post-trained for engineering-scale coding, multi-tool agent orchestration, and native vision understanding, keeping the same 2.4T-parameter base, 1M-token context, and $2/$6 per-million-token pricing. It debuted at #1 on the Code Arena: WebDev leaderboard, edging out Claude Opus 5. [Source](https://datanorth.ai/news/alibaba-releases-qwen3-8-max-0902)

## Other News
- **Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell sue Anthropic, naming Dario Amodei and Ben Mann personally** — The publishers accuse Anthropic of a "brazen campaign" of illegally torrenting and scraping tens of thousands of copyrighted songs (including "Uptown Funk" and "Hallelujah") to train Claude, seeking a jury trial and statutory damages up to $150,000 per composition. It's the first major music-industry suit to target Anthropic's founders individually rather than just the company. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/29/sony-music-warner-sue-anthropic-alleging-a-brazen-campaign-of-intellectual-property-theft/)
- **Thinking Machines Lab in talks to raise $1B at a $40B valuation** — Existing backer Accel is reportedly leading the round for Mira Murati's AI lab, with Nvidia potentially participating; the figure is below the $50B+ the company explored late last year, as its Tinker API and open-weight Inkling model drive over $100M in annualized revenue. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/accel-reportedly-in-talks-to-lead-1b-round-for-thinking-machines-at-40b-valuation/)
- **Google moves Gemini Notebook to compute-based usage limits** — Fixed daily caps on audio/video overviews, reports, and quizzes are being replaced by a flexible budget based on prompt complexity and feature use, refreshing every 5 hours until a weekly ceiling, mirroring the change Google made to the main Gemini app in May. [Source](https://gcn.com/gemini-notebook-switches-compute-based-limits/21309/)
