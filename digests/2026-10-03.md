## Other News
- **Anthropic launches $100M "Claude Frontier Academy" to train 10,000 enterprise AI engineers** — The program aims to train 10,000 "Frontier Deployed Engineers" by the end of 2027 through hands-on residencies modeled after medical education, addressing what Anthropic calls an outsized gap between a small pool of deeply skilled AI practitioners and the demand for enterprise AI deployment. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-frontier-academy)
- **Barclays scales Claude across global operations** — Barclays is rolling out Claude more broadly to modernize software development and customer service, targeting 50% developer adoption of Claude Code by end of 2026; a live Colleague Knowledge Assistant already supports over 16,000 employees with a million-plus searches, and Claude processes roughly 120,000 emails daily in the bank's Global Markets business. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/barclays-scales-claude)
