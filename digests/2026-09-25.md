# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-25

## Open Source
- **Liquid AI releases LFM2.5-VL-DSpark, a speculative-decoding draft model for its edge vision-language models** — The open-weight 280M-parameter draft model pairs with LFM2.5-VL-3B to speed up multimodal inference without changing output quality, delivering up to 3.13x decode speedups on-device and 2.66x on an H100, with day-one support in llama.cpp, MLX-VLM, and SGLang. Weights are available on Hugging Face in Safetensors and GGUF formats. [Source](https://huggingface.co/blog/LiquidAI/lfm2-5-vl-dspark)

## Other News
- **DeepSeek's annualized revenue run rate tops $1 billion after steep API price hikes** — Usage fees rose 2.3–4.5x last month, yet customer volume hasn't dropped, according to CEO Liang Wenfeng; the API business posted an 82.9% gross margin over the first seven months of the year. DeepSeek is now discussing a second funding round and a Shanghai STAR Market listing targeting roughly $7.5B at a $500B valuation. [Source](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9094958/deepseek-targets-1-billion-annual-revenue-amid-strong-market-demand)
- **Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic move to form a joint "Frontier AI Standards Agency"** — The three labs are courting former White House AI adviser Sriram Krishnan to lead a voluntary, FINRA-style self-regulatory body covering pre-deployment audits, incident reporting, and auditor qualification standards, with a possible launch in late 2026 or 2027 and no direct government oversight. [Source](https://www.bankinfosecurity.com/google-openai-anthropic-plan-frontier-ai-standards-body-a-32926)
- **Google preps first Project Suncatcher satellite carrying four TPUs for an October 1 launch** — The refrigerator-sized experimental spacecraft will ride a SpaceX rideshare mission from Vandenberg to test how Google's AI chips handle radiation, thermal extremes, and orbital cooling over roughly a year in low Earth orbit, an early step toward the company's vision of solar-powered orbital data centers. [Source](https://www.itechpost.com/articles/237428/20260925/google-sends-project-suncatcher-ai-data-center-space-bold-space-data-center-test.htm)
