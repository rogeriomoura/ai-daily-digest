# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-02

Quiet day for major model or open-source launches — today's notable development is in AI agent security.

## Other News
- **Nvidia launches Open Agent Safety Platform to contain rogue AI agents in milliseconds** — The platform pairs OpenShell, which limits what an agent can access (files, networks, apps), with Sentry, a watchdog running on Nvidia's BlueField-4 DPUs that quarantines an agent the instant it steps outside its assigned limits; because Sentry runs outside the agent's own environment, a compromised agent can't disable it. More than 100 organizations are working with the platform, including Anthropic, SpaceX AI, and JPMorganChase, and Nvidia says the system could have prevented the autonomous OpenAI-agent-swarm breach of Hugging Face disclosed earlier this year.
