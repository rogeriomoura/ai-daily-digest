# AI Daily Digest — 2026-08-26

## Open Source
- **Meta returns to open source with Muse Glimmer** — A 30B-parameter multimodal model distilled from Muse Spark, released under Apache 2.0 with ungated weights on Hugging Face. It's built for local agentic use on consumer hardware, pairing multi-step reasoning and tool use with a DFlash "drafter" model for faster generation. [Source](https://venturebeat.com/technology/meta-returns-to-open-source-with-muse-glimmer-an-apache-2-0-licensed-30b-parameter-ai-model-optimized-for-agents-available-now)

## Other News
- **OpenAI retires o3 from ChatGPT** — The model family exited ChatGPT today after a 90-day sunset period that began in May, consolidating OpenAI's reasoning lineup under the GPT-5 architecture (o3-pro remains available to Pro/Team/Enterprise/Edu users, and the change doesn't affect the API). [Source](https://forkast.news/openai-retired-o3-from-chatgpt-today-the-real-cost-is-the-churn-it-forces-on-everyone-else/)
- **Nvidia's Groq 3 LPX inference chip enters full production** — Built from Nvidia's $20B Groq acquisition, the dedicated inference accelerator now ships as a rack-scale extension to the Vera Rubin platform, handling latency-sensitive decoding for agentic workloads; Nebius, CoreWeave, and SpaceXAI are named as first customers. [Source](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/24/nvidias-dedicated-inference-accelerator-groq-3-lpx-enters-full-production-to-supercharge-ai-agents/)
- **Nvidia discloses Vera CPU architecture at Hot Chips 2026** — The 88-core, ARM-based "Olympus" server CPU breaks from chiplet designs with a monolithic die, spatial multithreading, and SOCAMM2 LPDDR5X memory up to 1.5TB; Nvidia claims a 1.8x speedup on agentic workloads paired with its Rubin GPUs. [Source](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/cpus/hot-chips-2026-nvidia-breaks-down-88-core-vera-cpu-spatial-multithreading-benchmarked-1-2-tb-s-socamm2-memory-agentic-workloads-detailed-and-more)
- **Japan adds another $944M to chipmaker Rapidus** — Japan's trade ministry will seek ¥150 billion in next year's budget for the state-backed foundry, aiming to boost its capital ahead of a planned 2027 launch of 2nm production as the country works to reduce reliance on TSMC. [Source](https://asia.nikkei.com/business/tech/semiconductors/japan-to-invest-additional-940m-in-homegrown-chipmaker-rapidus)
