# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-03

## Model Releases
- **Google ships Gemini 3.8 Flash, its third Flash update in six weeks** — The new workhorse model brings notable gains in software engineering, agentic tasks, and multi-step reasoning over 3.7 Flash, at the same introductory pricing ($0.75/$3.75 per million input/output tokens) until standard pricing kicks in January 2027. A locked-down "3.8 Flash Cyber" variant for cybersecurity tasks ships alongside it via Google's new limited-access Fairwind program for governments and trusted partners. [Source](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/3-8-flash-and-3-8-flash-cyber/)
- **Anthropic launches Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1** — Fable 5.1 targets ambitious coding, long-horizon agents, and document-heavy enterprise work, more than doubling Fable 5's Terminal-Bench-Science score (52.6% vs. 24.7%) and leading CursorBench 3.2 at 73.4% for agentic coding. Cheaper cache-read pricing brings typical workload costs down roughly 25% versus Fable 5; it's available now on the Claude Platform, AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Foundry. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1)

## Open Source
- **Alibaba open-weights Qwen3.8-27B under Apache 2.0** — A 27B-parameter vision-language model with a 262k-token context and a hybrid Gated DeltaNet/attention architecture, positioned for local coding assistants and private, on-device agent workflows. It posts sizable gains over Qwen3.6-27B, including 61.7% on SWE-Bench Pro. [Source](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B)

## Other News
- **AI-run vulnerability hunter AISLE finds 6 new CVEs in curl, including a 25-year-old bug** — AISLE's autonomous system led all researchers in curl's largest-ever CVE batch, uncovering authentication bypasses, memory corruption, and credential-leak issues in libcurl, which underpins billions of devices. The flood of AI-submitted reports has also strained curl's decade-old bug-bounty program. [Source](https://aisle.com/blog/aisle-discovered-six-curl-cves-after-openai-and-anthropic-found-zero)
- **Pentagon adds ChatGPT and Grok to its GenAI.mil platform** — The Defense Department is rolling out "ChatGPT Mil" and "Grok for Government" alongside the existing Gemini for Government, giving roughly 3 million military and civilian personnel access to frontier AI for planning, policy, logistics, and administrative work. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/the-pentagon-now-has-its-own-version-of-chatgpt-and-grok/)
