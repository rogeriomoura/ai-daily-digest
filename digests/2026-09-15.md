# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-15

## Open Source
- **OpenBMB releases VoxCPM2, a tokenizer-free multilingual TTS model** — The 2B-parameter diffusion-autoregressive model generates continuous speech representations directly (no discrete tokenizer step), supports 30 languages at 48kHz, and claims real-time streaming (RTF ~0.13 with Nano-vLLM acceleration). Weights and code are Apache-2.0 licensed and free for commercial use. [Source](https://github.com/OpenBMB/VoxCPM/)
