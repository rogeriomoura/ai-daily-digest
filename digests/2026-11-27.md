## Other News
- **US issues interim rule requiring licenses for AI chip exports to five countries, rescinds the AI Diffusion Rule** — Announced November 16 by the Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security and confirmed by BIS's own site plus multiple law-firm analyses (GT Law, Pillsbury, Steptoe, Morgan Lewis, KPMG), the interim final rule requires a license for exports, reexports, and in-country transfers of advanced computing chips to Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, and Brazil, which BIS says pose a higher-than-normal risk of chip diversion to China. It also imposes a new worldwide license requirement on data centers providing more than 12 million aggregate TPP of compute to Macau- or China-group-headquartered customers, with a carve-out for US-headquartered customers that file monthly usage reports. The rule formally rescinds the Biden-era January 2025 "AI Diffusion Rule." Singapore, Thailand, and Indonesia have each said they're reviewing the measure's scope and impact; shares of several Malaysian data-center firms fell sharply on the news. [Source](https://www.scmp.com/tech/article/3374639/us-tightens-ai-chip-export-curbs-close-chinas-overseas-data-centre-loophole)
