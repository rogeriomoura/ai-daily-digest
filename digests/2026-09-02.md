# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-02

## Model Releases
- **OpenAI previews "Astra," its first model to cross the Critical cybersecurity threshold** — In a "Path to Astra" report, OpenAI says its next flagship model can develop functional zero-day exploits and execute novel end-to-end attacks against hardened targets without human help, triggering the Critical tier of its Preparedness Framework. The company says Astra refuses 91.5% of cyber-jailbreak attempts in internal testing and will ship with restricted access before wider rollout; training was paused for two weeks in August to harden infrastructure after the July Hugging Face incident. [Source](https://openai.com/index/path-to-astra/)

## Other News
- **EU designates ChatGPT a "Very Large Online Search Engine" under the DSA** — The European Commission classified ChatGPT alongside Reddit and Roblox on August 31, citing nearly 160 million average monthly EU users, well above the 45 million threshold. OpenAI now has until the end of December to comply with DSA obligations on minor safety, algorithmic transparency, and independent risk audits, or face fines up to 6% of global turnover. [Source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-designates-chatgpt-reddit-roblox-under-digital-services-act)
- **Anthropic's "25% increase" to Claude Code limits draws backlash as a real-terms cut** — Anthropic confirmed it's permanently raising Claude Code's standard weekly limits by 25% for Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise plans starting September 14. But because a temporary 50% boost expires the same day, current users will see roughly 17% less capacity than they have today — a detail Anthropic initially buried in a reply thread before posting a fuller clarification. [Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-is-cutting-claude-codes-current-weekly-limits-by-17-percent/)
