# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-08

## Model Releases
- **Anthropic ships Claude Haiku 5.5, its cheapest and fastest small model yet** — Haiku 5.5 adds an adjustable effort setting, large benchmark jumps over Haiku 4.5 (e.g. Terminal-Bench 4.0 39.2% vs. 0.0%, OSWorld 2.1 offline 72.4% vs. 15.7%), and runs roughly 75% cheaper on average ($0.10/$0.50 per MTok input, $0.50/$2.50 output for prompts under 100k tokens). Anthropic also cut Sonnet 5.5's cache-read price in half (to $0.10/MTok) and introduced monthly API credits for Claude Max and Team subscribers. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-haiku-5-5)
- **Mistral opens a public preview of Mistral Large 4** — The French lab's largest model to date is a multimodal mixture-of-experts system with roughly 1.05 trillion total parameters (49B active) and a 1M-token context window. It's available now via API preview while training continues; open weights are planned for later this month pending safety review. [Source](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/)
- **OpenAI rolls out GPT-6 with "Intelligent UI" in ChatGPT** — The update lets ChatGPT automatically mix text with interactive elements (calculators, visualizations, mini-apps) when that suits the query better than plain text, falling back to text otherwise. Paid tiers get access first, with Free and Go users following. [Source](https://openai.com/index/devday-2026-recap/)

## Other News
- **Meta and Sierra propose an open "Personal Agent Protocol" for agent-to-business interactions** — The draft standard (v0.1) aims to let personal AI agents authenticate and act on a user's behalf when dealing with businesses — bookings, orders, support — with Walmart, Shopify, Stripe, Rocket, Genesys, and Instinct named as founding partners. It's open for any vendor to implement rather than tied to Meta or Sierra. [Source](https://letsdatascience.com/news/meta-and-sierra-introduce-personal-agent-protocol-for-busine-13aa4537)
- **OpenAI and Atlassian expand their partnership** — Atlassian will build agents across its platform and Rovo on OpenAI's frontier models, with expanded access to GPT-6 Astra and the GPT-5.6 series. [Source](https://openai.com/index/atlassian-partnership/)
