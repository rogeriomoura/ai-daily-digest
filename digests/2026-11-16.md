## Other News
- **Anthropic launches Claude Meetings, Whiteboards, and Sheets, and makes Claude Data generally available** — Announced November 10 on Anthropic's blog and widely reported (TechCrunch, VentureBeat, Betanews, and others), the release pushes Claude further into office-suite territory: Claude Meetings joins video calls (Google Meet only at launch) to work on tasks while a call is in progress; Claude Whiteboards is a shared visual brainstorming space that can build alongside a Meetings call; and Claude Sheets lets users build and edit spreadsheets with Claude, which an Anthropic product lead reportedly described as "fairly basic" rather than aimed at financial modeling. Claude Data (formerly Dashboards) moves from beta to general availability. Meetings, Whiteboards, and Sheets are in beta on paid plans; outlets differ on exact default-on settings for Enterprise admins. Coverage frames this as a direct push against Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace. [Source](https://venturebeat.com/ai/claude-deepens-its-enterprise-push-with-document-generation-taking-the-ai)
