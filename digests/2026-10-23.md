# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-23

A quiet day for new model drops or open-source breakouts, but a strong day for AI research and infrastructure news.

## Other News
- **Microsoft Research uses AI agents to crack a 31-year-old math conjecture** — Microsoft announced on October 21 that researcher Xinzhi Zhang and collaborator Zhao Song proved the Kannan–Lovász–Simonovits (KLS) conjecture, a long-standing problem in high-dimensional geometry underlying randomized-sampling algorithms, using a human-directed agentic AI process that explored over 100 candidate approaches before arriving at the criterion behind the final 140-page proof. [Source](https://hermes-ai.net/news/microsoft-solves-a-31-year-old-math-problem-using-ai-agents/)
- **MIT researchers leave Claude agents alone on a virtual island — they start playing games** — In a not-yet-peer-reviewed study reported this week, 13 copies of a Claude Opus 4.7-powered coding agent were given bodies in a simulated world with no assigned task for 30 hours each; they invented activities like basketball and "the floor is lava," and agents with play experience outperformed fresh copies on later object-transport tasks. Researchers caution it's unclear whether the agents were "really" playing or role-playing play. [Source](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/10/19/1147095/ai-stranded-claude-virtual-desert-island-play/)
- **OpenAI reportedly in talks for a $30B+ round near a $1.25–1.4T valuation, confidential IPO filing in parallel** — Multiple outlets report OpenAI is pursuing a bridge funding round in lieu of an imminent IPO, with Altman having said the company won't go public in 2026; terms are described as still in flux and unconfirmed by OpenAI itself. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/openai-working-on-new-30b-raise-at-1-25t-valuation-ahead-of-ipo/)
- **Nvidia and Google Cloud announce general availability of Vera Rubin infrastructure at GTC Berlin** — At Nvidia's GTC Berlin event (Oct 20–22), Jensen Huang's keynote highlighted the modular "Vera Rubin DSK Pod" data-center design, alongside a joint announcement with Google Cloud bringing Vera Rubin infrastructure to general availability and a broader sovereign-cloud push across Europe, the Nordics, Africa, and Central Asia. [Source](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/gtc-berlin-2026-news/)
