# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-31

Google pushed out a cybersecurity-focused frontier model, while OpenAI's fundraising and its price war with Anthropic both made headlines amid a broader tech-stock selloff.

## Model Releases
- **Google unveils Gemini 4 Carbon, a cybersecurity-focused frontier model** — Announced October 28 as a follow-up to Gemini 4 Argon from two weeks earlier, Carbon tops the updated Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 (score of 59, edging out Claude Opus 5.5) and reports strong marks on security benchmarks: 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro (vulnerability discovery) and 71.0% on CWE-bench (patching). Access starts narrowly with cybersecurity defenders through Google's Fairwind Program before a wider rollout to developers and consumers. [Source](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/google-launches-gemini-4-carbon-ai-model-with-cybersecurity-focus-2)

## Other News
- **OpenAI reportedly trims its funding-round valuation target to $1.25 trillion amid an AI stock selloff** — Bloomberg reported (~October 22, still developing as of this week) that OpenAI lowered its target for an up-to-$30B round — led by Coatue and UAE investors including MGX — from an earlier $1.4 trillion pitch to $1.25 trillion pre-money. Coverage ties the lower target to a broader tech selloff driven by doubts about AI infrastructure spending and financing costs; terms remain unfinalized and OpenAI has not commented. [Source](https://www.benzinga.com/node/62552583)
- **OpenAI's steep enterprise discounts are pulling large customers away from Anthropic** — Per reporting from The Information, OpenAI has used discounts above 30% (and in some accounts up to 90%) to win business, with Replit reportedly moving a $100M+ annual contract and Rippling's coding-work share shifting sharply toward OpenAI. Anthropic, by contrast, reportedly caps its own enterprise discounts and ends them once contracted token volumes run out. Figures vary by outlet and aren't independently verified. [Source](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-discounts-anthropic-customers-replit-rippling)
