# AI Daily Digest — 2026-08-28

## Model Releases
- **Mystery "Ox Alpha" model unmasked as Z.ai's GLM-5.3-Flash** — Z.ai confirmed that the stealth model covered in yesterday's digest was an anonymous preview of GLM-5.3-Flash, a 320B-parameter (18B active) natively multimodal MoE released under the MIT license with weights on Hugging Face. It's the first open-weight frontier model to combine sparse and linear attention, cutting attention compute and KV-cache size by roughly 3x and 4.4x versus GLM-5.3 while supporting a 1M-token input context. [Source](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/26/z-ai-open-sources-ox-alpha-model-as-glm-5-3-flash/)

## Open Source
- **DeepSeek Harness becomes GitHub's fastest-growing repo ever** — DeepSeek's MIT-licensed, Node.js coding-agent harness treats its interface, tool calls, and agent loop as swappable plugins and works with any model provider (DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, Bedrock, Azure, Google) rather than locking users into one. Released as a developer preview around August 21, it topped 20,000 stars within an hour and has since passed 200,000, gaining roughly 28,000 in the past week alone. [Source](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/deepseek-harness-coding-agent)

## Other News
- **Federal judge rules Pentagon's "supply chain risk" label on Anthropic was unlawful** — U.S. District Judge Rita Lin found the Department of Defense retaliated against Anthropic for refusing unrestricted military use of Claude, violating the First and Fifth Amendments, and ordered the designation removed. The ruling followed Anthropic's March lawsuit calling the designation an "unlawful campaign of retaliation"; the government is expected to appeal. [Source](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/28/nx-s1-5947761/judge-pentagon-anthropic-illegal)
- **Anthropic previews a standard for AI agents to control lab and factory hardware** — The Model Hardware Standard (MHS), likened to "a USB-C cord" for agent-to-device communication, lets Claude or any other model operate microscopes, liquid handlers, and robotic arms in parallel for tasks like drug-discovery experiments or quantum-computer calibration. It's launching as a research preview with select science and manufacturing partners, is model-agnostic, and Anthropic says it plans to open-source it later. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-hardware-standard-research-preview)
- **Nvidia in talks to back Perplexity at $30B+ valuation** — The prospective round would mark over a 50% jump from Perplexity's roughly $20B valuation a year ago, as the company's annualized revenue climbed past $750M (from under $250M in January), partly driven by its "Perplexity Computer" AI agent product. Neither the investment nor valuation has been finalized. [Source](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/nvidia-discusses-perplexity-investment-30-billion-plus-valuation-considered-tech-licensing-deal)
