# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-19

A quiet day for major new model releases — none of the big labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Mistral, xAI, DeepSeek, Qwen) published anything new and independently verifiable today. Community open-source events and AI policy were the main threads.

## Open Source
- **Hugging Face kicks off its 10-day Open Source AI Week in San Francisco** — The program opened October 16 with "Open Together," a free community meetup at The Midway featuring roughly 36 live demos from open-source builders, sponsored by Arcee AI and Reflection AI, with MiniMax joining as a host. The broader Open Source AI Week runs through October 25 with talks, workshops, and hackathons across the Bay Area. [Source](https://digg.com/ai/8ti4zb54)

## Other News
- **Anthropic quietly pitches its own frontier AI safety bill to Capitol Hill lawmakers** — Politico reported October 16, citing three people familiar with the outreach, that Anthropic representatives have been informally shopping draft federal legislation on frontier-model safety and security to members of Congress. The move comes as competing AI safety bills (from Sen. Schiff and a bipartisan Klobuchar-Cruz-Thune effort) also circulate on the Hill, with no consensus yet on preemption or enforcement. [Source](https://thedailyguardian.com/business/anthropic-quietly-pitches-its-own-ai-safety-bill-to-lawmakers-politico-reports-2-766716/)
- **Apple ships a new Apple TV 4K built around "Siri AI," its rebuilt assistant** — Announced October 13, the $299 fourth-generation device adds far-field microphones for hands-free, conversational Siri interaction and a full-screen AI experience, plus Apple Intelligence features like camera-activity descriptions for compatible smart home cameras. The Siri AI software ships as a tvOS 27.2 beta with a limited language/country list, so full functionality may lag the November 13 hardware launch. [Source](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-unveils-the-new-apple-tv-4k/)
