## Model Releases
- **Alibaba ships Qwen4-Max to general availability** — Announced November 16 via Alibaba Cloud's own press release (not previously covered here), Qwen4-Max is a closed, API-only flagship combining text, image/video understanding, reasoning, web search, and tool calling, priced at roughly $1.50/$4.50 per million input/output tokens. Alibaba claims it beats Qwen3.8-Max while using up to ~50% fewer tokens — an unverified, vendor-reported figure — and says smaller Qwen4 models will be open-sourced later, with no date given. [Source](https://www.alibabacloud.com/en/press-room/alibaba-unveils-qwen4-max)
