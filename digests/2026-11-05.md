# AI Daily Digest — 2026-11-05

Still no clearly-sourced model or open-source release today, but a busy few days for AI-adjacent M&A and a notable safety warning from Anthropic: it says it's getting close to AI that can meaningfully improve itself.

## Other News
- **Anthropic says it may be nearing recursive self-improvement (RSI) capability** — In a blog post from Anthropic Institute co-founder Jack Clark, reported around November 2, Anthropic said it believes the industry is closer than previously expected to AI systems capable of recursively improving themselves, and that full RSI could raise the risk of humans losing control of AI systems. The post reiterated that Anthropic would only slow or pause development if rival frontier labs did so too, in a verifiable way. The warning landed shortly after Anthropic's IPO paperwork became public, which several outlets noted as notable timing. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/anthropic-warns-that-its-very-close-to-rsi-capability-aka-self-building-ai/)
- **Meta acquires AI coding startup Conductor** — Reported November 4, first by Axios and confirmed to TechCrunch, Meta bought Conductor, a 2024-founded startup (CEO Charlie Holtz, CTO Jackson de Campos) whose platform runs multiple AI coding agents — including Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor — in parallel workspaces for review and merging. Conductor had raised a $22M Series A from Spark and Matrix in March 2026; deal terms weren't disclosed. The move puts Meta in more direct competition with OpenAI and others for ownership of agent-orchestration tooling for developers. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/04/meta-acquires-ai-coding-startup-conductor/)
- **CrowdStrike to acquire Fiddler AI and Dreadnode for a reported ~$495M** — Announced November 3 at Fal.Con Europe in Barcelona, CrowdStrike is buying Fiddler AI (agent observability/explainability, to become "Falcon Observe") and Dreadnode (pre-deployment AI security testing, feeding "Falcon Guardian"). CrowdStrike didn't disclose price; TechCrunch cited the Wall Street Journal for a roughly $400M/$95M split between the two deals. CrowdStrike also launched an "AI Kill Switch" inside Falcon Guardian, letting security admins shut down AI agents acting outside their intended instructions. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/crowdstrike-to-shell-out-nearly-500m-for-2-ai-security-startups-as-the-market-for-securing-agents-takes-off/)
