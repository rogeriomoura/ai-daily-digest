# AI Daily Digest — 2026-11-06

Another quiet stretch for new model and open-source releases, but the AI-stock story took a sharp turn this week: a broad selloff tied to Tuesday's US midterms hit AI-adjacent stocks hard, with knock-on doubts about the IPO timelines for both OpenAI and Anthropic.

## Other News
- **Midterm-linked AI stock selloff wipes out an estimated $2.5T, hits SoftBank hardest** — Following Tuesday's (November 3) US midterm elections, global markets sold off sharply this week on fears that the results could bring tighter AI regulation and that the industry's spending boom is getting harder to justify; CNN put US market losses at roughly $2.5 trillion. SoftBank was among the hardest hit, with shares tumbling further and the cost of insuring its debt against default reportedly jumping to a four-year high; Reuters noted investors are also weighing reports that OpenAI could push its own IPO out to 2027. [Source](https://cnn.com/2026/11/05/markets/asian-markets-us-rout-ai-investment-intl-hnk)
- **Market turmoil raises doubts about Anthropic's Nov 9 IPO marketing start** — Anthropic had been tracking toward launching IPO marketing the week of November 9 and pricing the week of November 16, per Renaissance Capital. But secondary reporting of Reuters/WSJ coverage says some investors are now pushing for a lower valuation and doubt Anthropic can start marketing on that schedule given this week's broader AI-stock rout; Anthropic has not commented. [Source](https://www.renaissancecapital.com/IPO-Center/Winners-Losers/1570)
