# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-23

## Model Releases
- **Anthropic launches Claude Opus 5.5, matching Fable 5.1 at 40% lower cost** — The new model surpasses Fable 5.1 on agentic coding, knowledge work, computer use, chart recognition, and multidisciplinary reasoning benchmarks while cutting typical workload costs 40% versus Opus 5 (now $4/$20 per MTok, cache reads down 60% to $0.20/MTok) and boosting output speed over 30%; Anthropic says Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 are coming within weeks. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5)
- **OpenAI ships GPT-6 Sol and Luna at roughly half the price of GPT-5.6** — Sol ($2/$10 per million tokens) targets everyday reasoning work and makes about half as many mistakes as GPT-5.6 Sol, while Luna ($0.10/$0.50) is a faster, cheaper tier reaching free ChatGPT desktop users; both inherit factuality, coding, and computer-use gains developed for GPT-6 Astra. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/openai-launches-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/)

## Open Source

[Source](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/22/xiaomi-introduces-mimo-v2-6-series-open-source-ai-model-family/)
- **Alphabet's Intrinsic open-sources Intrinsic Core, its industrial robotics stack, under Apache 2.0** — Unveiled at ROSCon 2026, the ROS-compatible release packages the real-time control, Nvidia FoundationPose-based pose estimation, motion/grasp planning, and simulation tooling Intrinsic uses in live manufacturing deployments into reusable building blocks, alongside an open CNC machine-tending reference design. [Source](https://www.intrinsic.ai/blog/posts/introducing-intrinsic-core)

[Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/22/altman-amodei-unga-ai-safety.html)
