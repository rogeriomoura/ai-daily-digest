# AI Daily Digest — 2026-11-14

A lighter news day: Alibaba opened a time-limited preview of its next flagship Qwen model, and OpenAI confirmed a ChatGPT voice-mode retirement date first flagged earlier this week.

## Model Releases
- **Alibaba opens a two-week preview of Qwen4-Max** — Starting November 11 (10:00 CST) through November 25, 2026, Alibaba made "Qwen4-Max-Preview" available exclusively through its Token Plan subscription (no pay-as-you-go API access), per multiple outlets (Decrypt, AlphaSignal, AIbase, and others). It supports a 1M-token context window with up to 393,216 tokens of combined output, accepts text/image/video input, and reasons before answering; Alibaba has published no benchmark table, model card, parameter count, or weights, so performance claims are unverified and the preview model ID will stop working once the window closes. [Source](https://decrypt.co/364948)

## Other News
- **OpenAI confirms December 11 retirement of ChatGPT's Advanced and Standard Voice modes** — Per OpenAI's help-center article and coverage from TechRadar, Android Authority, and others, both legacy voice modes are being retired on December 11, 2026, with affected users automatically switched to "Live Voice." Live video and screen-sharing will keep running on Advanced Voice until Live Voice supports them, and custom-GPT voice follows the same December 11 cutoff (February 11, 2027 for Enterprise/FedRAMP workspaces with an approved deferral). OpenAI frames the change as consolidating around its newer voice technology. [Source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001638)
