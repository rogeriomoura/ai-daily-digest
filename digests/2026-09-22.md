# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-22

## Model Releases
- **xAI ships Grok 4.7, a 2.1T-parameter model trained partly on SpaceX engineering data** — Released Monday at the same $2/$6 per million input/output token pricing and 500K context window as Grok 4.6, the model is up 40% in parameter count and spends longer double-checking hard answers; xAI also folded in Starlink telemetry, manufacturing records, and engineering failure logs to improve reasoning about physical systems. It's live now in the Grok app, Cursor, Grok Build, GitHub Copilot, and the xAI API with no waitlist. [Source](https://decrypt.co/378824/xai-launches-grok-4-7)

## Open Source
- **Cactus Compute releases Needle, a 2-bit foundation model that fits in 8–29MB** — Built on a Simple Attention Network rather than a scaled-down transformer, Needle targets tool-calling, structured extraction, and embeddings directly on phones, wearables, and microcontrollers (running on an ESP32-S3 in ~11MB of RAM), beating models 10x its size on mobile tool calls despite trading away general chat ability. Weights, engine, and training code are open on GitHub and Hugging Face. [Source](https://github.com/cactus-compute/needle)

## Other News
- **Google rolls out real-time voice conversations and new study tools in Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM)** — The mobile app now supports source-grounded voice chat in nearly 100 languages, plus a lecture audio recorder and interactive reports combining quizzes, flashcards, and infographics; voice is rolling out first to Google AI Ultra subscribers, with US students getting a free year of Google AI Pro through December 31. [Source](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/new-study-tools-september-2026/)
