# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-10

Quiet day for new model or open-source launches — today's notable developments are in AI safety and policy.

## Other News
- **OpenAI disrupts Iranian "Bogus Bylines" influence operation** — OpenAI banned a cluster of ChatGPT accounts behind a campaign that invented seven fake Western journalist personas to push roughly 100 articles critical of the US war against Iran across about a dozen outlets. OpenAI said the activity "appeared consistent with a commercial actor running a for-hire influence campaign" rather than confirmed state backing. [Source](https://www.engadget.com/cybersecurity/openai-shut-down-an-iranian-influence-op-that-used-chatgpt-to-generate-bogus-news-articles-202526662.html)
- **Common Sense Media rates ChatGPT for Teens "Unacceptable Risk"** — The nonprofit's Youth AI Safety Institute ran over 4,000 prompts on accounts registered as 13- to 17-year-olds and found crisis-hotline referrals and depression-hotline mentions dropped sharply after the teen launch, with parental alerts often failing to reach parents during long self-harm conversations. It recommends OpenAI restrict ChatGPT to 18+ until the gaps are fixed; OpenAI disputes the findings. [Source](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/press-releases/chatgpt-for-teens-poses-unacceptable-risk-to-kids-common-sense-media-finds)
- **Anthropic launches a free vulnerability scanner for open-source projects** — As part of a broader "Cyber Mission" with 11 founding partners, Anthropic's new OSS Scanner uses its frontier models to periodically scan opted-in open-source projects at no cost, producing a proof-of-concept, explanation, and suggested fix for each finding. It launches alongside a Critical Infrastructure Defense Program and builds on the company's earlier Project Glasswing work. [Source](https://siliconangle.com/2026/10/08/anthropic-launches-critical-infrastructure-program-and-free-oss-scanner-for-open-source/)
