# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-18

## Open Source
- **DeskcommCRM trends as a self-hosted, open-source AI sales OS** — The MCP-ready, multi-tenant CRM pairs native AI agents with WhatsApp (via WAHA) to answer chat-driven sales inquiries, qualify leads, and move them through the pipeline, positioning itself as an open alternative to Kommo, Octadesk, and Intercom; it supports Anthropic, OpenAI, or OpenRouter as the underlying model provider. [Source](https://github.com/joel299/DeskcommCRM)

## Other News
- **Anthropic proposes three metrics to track AI "self-improvement" at frontier labs** — The metrics — AI-led R&D share, agent oversight, and compute allocation — aim to give the public visibility into how much AI development is automating itself; Anthropic disclosed that Claude now leads 26% of its own internal model R&D, up from under 1% earlier in 2026, and published July–August figures as a baseline. The move follows CEO Dario Amodei's call for the industry to coordinate on slowing frontier development. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/institute/measuring-pace-of-ai-development)
- **OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind confirm talks toward a joint AI standards body** — The three labs have met regularly since July on a self-regulatory model proposed by DeepMind's Demis Hassabis, styled after FINRA's oversight of U.S. brokerages; OpenAI's Sam Altman said he favors an industry-run testing and auditing body without government backing, while FTC Chairman Andrew Ferguson has voiced suspicion that labs are seeking antitrust cover under a "safety cooperation" banner. [Source](https://techxplore.com/news/2026-09-openai-anthropic-google-ai-standards.html)
- **OpenAI begins piloting "Sponsored Agents" inside ChatGPT** — Labeled, business-run AI agents now appear in relevant conversations and open into a separate chat built by the brand; Wayfair (furniture Q&A) and Angi (home-service contractor matching) are the first advertisers, with HubSpot and Shopify joining as the first CRM and e-commerce integration partners. [Source](https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/openai-tests-sponsored-agents-adds-ai-tools-chatgpt-advertisers)
