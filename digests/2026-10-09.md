# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-09

Quiet day for new model or open-source launches — today's notable developments are a cluster of Google product rollouts.

## Other News
- **Google brings agentic AI to Gemini, starting with businesses** — At a Google Cloud event, Google unveiled a unified agent in Gemini Enterprise that takes objectives rather than step-by-step instructions, plans its own work, and connects to a business's internal systems and tools (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, Jira, Snowflake, or any MCP server). It picks a model per task by default but lets users choose one, including third-party models starting with Anthropic's Claude; Sundar Pichai said nearly 90% of Fortune 100 companies now use Gemini Enterprise. Businesses get access first, with consumers to follow. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/google-brings-agentic-ai-to-gemini-starting-with-businesses/)
- **Google opens its SynthID AI-content detector to the public** — The standalone SynthID verification site, previously limited to journalists, researchers, and media professionals, now lets anyone upload an image, video, or audio file to check for Google's invisible AI watermark. Google says it has watermarked over 10 billion pieces of content, but the tool only catches Google-made watermarks — a clean result doesn't prove content is authentic. [Source](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/google-just-launched-a-free-ai-detector-for-music-videos-and-images-heres-how-synthid-works-and-what-it-can-and-cant-tell-you)
- **Google Labs launches Playground, a prompt-to-game builder** — The experimental browser tool lets anyone describe a game in plain language — genre, mechanics, 2D/3D, single- or multiplayer — and generates a playable browser game using Gemini, Nano Banana, and Lyria models, with no coding required. It's live now at playground.google for US users 18+, gated by weekly creation limits, with a planned Unity Spark integration for more advanced 3D games. [Source](https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/10/google-unveils-playground-an-ai-platform-that-lets-anyone-create-games-without-coding/)
