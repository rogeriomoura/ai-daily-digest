# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-30

Mistral finally shipped the open weights it had been promising all month, while two of the biggest private AI labs put hard numbers on just how expensive this buildout has gotten.

## Model Releases
- **Mistral ships open weights for Large 4 ("Le Chonk")** — Posted October 29, the release follows the October 6 public preview with roughly three more weeks of reinforcement learning baked in. Weights (~1 trillion total / ~49B active parameters, FP8 and NVFP4 checkpoints) are on Hugging Face under a modified, non-Apache license that requires a separate commercial license for model-as-a-service use; the model remains available hosted via Mistral Studio, the Mistral API, and OpenRouter. [Source](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4-oss/)

## Other News
- **Meta moves to raise up to $95B for AI infrastructure, with Nvidia and AMD eyeing stakes** — Announced October 26, Meta launched roughly $35B in public common and convertible-preferred stock offerings, with up to $60B more planned via an at-the-market program starting Q1 2027. Meta's filing noted Nvidia and AMD (and Broadcom) expressed non-binding interest in buying up to $5B combined of its stock; Meta shares dipped slightly on the news. [Source](https://www.freepressjournal.in/tech/nvidia-amd-to-invest-4-billion-in-meta-as-it-raises-95-billion-for-ai-infrastructure)
- **Anthropic's IPO paperwork shows an $80.7B first-half net loss — but it's mostly accounting, not cash burn** — Figures from Anthropic's draft S-1 (first reported ~October 21–22, following its October 26 confidential filing) show H1 2026 revenue of $16.3B against an $80.7B net loss; about $72B of that is a non-cash charge from fair-value changes in convertible notes as Anthropic's valuation climbed. Compute and infrastructure spend roughly quadrupled year-over-year to near $9B for the half. [Source](https://www.thewrap.com/industry-news/tech/anthropic-ipo-filing-80-billion-loss-revenue/)
