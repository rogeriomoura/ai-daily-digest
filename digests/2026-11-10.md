# AI Daily Digest — 2026-11-10

The big AI story this week keeps being Wall Street, not model benchmarks: Anthropic's IPO moved from "will investors show up" to an actual price range. On the model side, Thinking Machines Lab and a smaller open-weights shop both put out new checkpoints this week.

## Model Releases
- **Thinking Machines Lab ships Inkling 2 Small, a 594B-parameter MoE model (weights still closed)** — Reported November 5 by several outlets (The Decoder, Digg, Let's Data Science), Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Small — a 594-billion-parameter, 17-billion-active mixture-of-experts model with image input and variable "effort" — on its Tinker platform. Weights aren't public yet; the lab says it plans to open them under Apache 2.0 next week after safety red-teaming. Note this is a different, larger model from "Inkling-Small" (276B/12B), the earlier Thinking Machines checkpoint that Cognition's SWE-2 Flash was built on (covered here November 9). Benchmark comparisons (e.g., an Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index score of 31 vs. Mistral Large 4's 26) are a mix of third-party and company-selected figures. [Source](https://the-decoder.com/thinking-machines-bets-on-efficiency-over-size-with-its-second-model-inkling-small/)

## Open Source
- **Ricursive releases Ripple-65B Preview, a 65B open-weight coding model under Apache 2.0** — Published to Hugging Face on November 4 and reported by multiple outlets (Let's Data Science, Mer.vin, Digital Applied), Ripple-65B Preview is a 65B-parameter dense transformer (80 layers, 1M-token context) trained on 12T tokens, released with instruct, base, NVFP4, and speculative-decoding-drafter variants. Ricursive says it cost about $32M to build and claims a Terminal-Bench 2.1 score of 80.52, which it frames as a lead among US open-weight coding models — both the cost and benchmark figures are the company's own and haven't been independently verified. [Source](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ripple-65b-preview-open-weight-model)

## Other News
- **Anthropic sets $70–$78/share IPO price range after retracting an earlier draft filing** — Reported November 9–10 by TechCrunch and CNBC, Anthropic is moving ahead with a Nasdaq listing (ticker "ANT") of roughly 825 million shares priced at $70–$78, which would raise up to about $64B at the top of the range. An earlier draft filing that briefly reached banks was pulled over pricing inaccuracies before the corrected range went out. The company reports run-rate revenue of about $81B as of October 31, up roughly $10B from September 30. Some institutional investors are reportedly still pushing for a lower valuation than the ~$1.5T figure discussed last week, following a broader AI-stock selloff and a Reuters report (Nov 5) that investors doubted the roadshow could hold its original schedule. [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-price-range-of-70-to-78-in-upcoming-ipo.html)
- **Anthropic strikes its first paid news-licensing deal, with the Associated Press** — Reported November 4 by Adweek (citing two people familiar with the matter, not yet confirmed on the record by either company), Anthropic licensed AP election data and attribution for Claude around the US midterms — its first paid content deal with a digital news publisher, following OpenAI's dozen-plus such deals. Terms weren't disclosed, and Adweek cautioned it isn't necessarily a sign of a broader licensing strategy shift. [Source](https://www.adweek.com/media/anthropic-licensing-deal-ap-election/)
