# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-15

## Model Releases
- **Google's Gemini 4 Argon reaches general availability for developers and businesses** — First announced September 30 for select cybersecurity partners, Argon rolled out more broadly on October 13 via the Gemini API, Google AI Studio, and Antigravity, alongside access in the Gemini app for AI Pro/Ultra subscribers. The model's output window grew to 1M tokens (up from 64K), aimed at longer autonomous coding and debugging sessions; Google says its own engineers are already using it for codebase migrations. Benchmark comparisons against GPT-6 Astra and Anthropic's models are Google's own and not independently verified. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/30/google-releases-gemini-4-argon-called-its-most-powerful-model-yet/)

## Open Source
- **NVIDIA open-sources its full Run:ai platform as part of DSX AI Factory** — The release splits into four independently adoptable modules: KAI Scheduler (GPU-aware Kubernetes scheduling), a GPU Fractioning Operator for CUDA-native GPU sharing, KAI Resource Management for organizational quotas, and a Workload Map contract. Eight DSX ecosystem partners — including Crusoe, Mirantis, Nebius, and Spectro Cloud — are already integrating it. NVIDIA claims it lets AI factories run up to 40% more GPUs within a fixed power budget, a vendor figure not independently confirmed. [Source](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-runai-is-now-open-source-as-part-of-nvidia-dsx-ai-factory-platform/)

## Other News
- **Anthropic warns small AI models could soon self-replicate across insecure smart devices** — In research published October 12, Anthropic ran Google's Gemma4-26B-A4B model across 15 cheap ($55) Android TV boxes, achieving only about 0.39 tokens/second and no autonomous spread — but it argues that if small-model efficiency keeps improving at its historical rate, the capability needed for self-replication across weakly secured consumer devices (citing smart TVs and ovens) could arrive by the end of 2026. Anthropic notes such a system would be hard to shut down since there's no central off-switch, while stressing that a rogue self-replicating model does not exist today. [Source](https://gizmodo.com/ai-could-soon-feed-on-smart-devices-like-a-digital-slime-mold-anthropic-warns-2000825365)
