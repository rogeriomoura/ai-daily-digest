# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-30

## Model Releases
- **OpenAI DevDay 2026: "Dots" always-on agents, a Pro 500 tier, and an 8x-faster Ultrafast speed tier** — At Fort Mason in San Francisco on September 29, Sam Altman unveiled Dots, always-on agents built on GPT-6 Astra that get their own cloud computer, connect to 4,000+ apps, and can be messaged from ChatGPT, Slack, or Teams, rolling out first to Pro and Business Premium users. OpenAI also launched Pro 500 (its highest-usage subscription tier) and Ultrafast, a premium speed tier generating tokens up to 8x faster in Codex and 6x faster via the API, plus plugin extensions that let developers build full app-like experiences (editors, dashboards) inside ChatGPT and Codex. [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/openai-devday-2026-live-updates.html)

## Other News
- **Amazon Mechanical Turk shuts down after 21 years** — AWS closed the crowdsourcing platform on September 30, ending a service that once connected 500,000+ workers to micro-tasks like image tagging and transcription and that became a foundational source of human-labeled data for early AI research. Amazon gave no official reason, but the platform had been losing ground to newer AI-focused data-labeling and human-feedback firms like Scale AI, Mercor, and Prolific, and a 2023 study found nearly half of MTurk workers were already using AI to complete their assigned tasks. [Source](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-amazon-mechanical-turk-shutdown/)
