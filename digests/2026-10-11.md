# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-11

Quiet day for major model launches — today's notable developments are both open-source releases.

## Open Source
- **Google DeepMind releases EmbeddingGemma 2, an open multimodal embedding model** — Built on the Gemma 4 architecture, the 740M-parameter model places text, code, images, audio, and video into a single 768-dimensional vector space, split across a 270M text model, 170M vision encoder, and 300M audio encoder that developers can load independently. It's Apache 2.0 licensed and designed for on-device use, reportedly running in roughly 191MB–567MB of RAM depending on configuration. [Source](https://letsdatascience.com/news/deepmind-releases-embeddinggemma-2-for-edge-search-fbcc7c51)
- **JetBrains ships Mellum2.1 Thinking, an open-weight coding-agent model** — The 12B mixture-of-experts model (2.5B active parameters, 131K context) is aimed at agentic coding tasks, with reinforcement learning — run across millions of sandboxed environments — making up the bulk of post-training rather than a short final step. It's released under Apache 2.0 with weights on Hugging Face; JetBrains cites a large SWE-bench Verified gain over the original Mellum2, though the figures are vendor-reported and not yet independently verified. [Source](https://www.llmreference.com/model/mellum2-1-12b-thinking)
