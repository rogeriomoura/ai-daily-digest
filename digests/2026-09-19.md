# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-19

## Model Releases
- **SpaceXAI ships Grok Voice Transcribe 2.0** — The new streaming speech-to-text model ranks #1 for accuracy among 32 models on Artificial Analysis's leaderboard (2.7% word error rate on final transcripts), roughly halving errors versus Grok Voice Transcribe 1.0 at the same price; multilingual short-phrase WER drops from 20.6% to 6.8%. It adds speaker diarization, word-level timestamps, and multichannel transcription of up to eight channels. [Source](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/18/spacexai-releases-grok-voice-transcribe-2-0/)

## Open Source
- **OpenSpec crosses 69k GitHub stars pushing spec-driven development for coding agents** — Fission-AI's tool has developers and AI coding assistants (Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and 30+ others) agree on a written spec before any code is generated, turning that spec into the source of truth so implementation changes stay reviewable and deterministic rather than opaque prompt-to-code output. [Source](https://github.com/Fission-AI/OpenSpec)

## Other News
- **Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and SpaceXAI hit with a federal antitrust suit over Amodei's "pace the frontier" plea** — Filed in the Northern District of California on Friday, the Sherman Act Section 1 complaint argues that Dario Amodei's public call for industry-wide coordination to slow frontier AI development — quickly echoed by Sam Altman, Elon Musk, and Demis Hassabis — amounted to an illegal agreement among competitors not to compete on safety-paced capability. Plaintiffs are seeking class-action status. [Source](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/anthropic-openai-spacexai-google-face-federal-antitrust-lawsuit-over-calls-to-slowdown-ai-development)
- **100+ AI researchers, including Geoffrey Hinton, demand truly independent safety evaluators** — A public letter organized by the AI Evaluator Forum, with signers from Stanford, Johns Hopkins, and METR, says third-party evaluators of frontier models currently lack the independence, resources, and legal protection needed for credible safety testing. It calls on labs to grant embedded evaluators employee-level system access, editorial control over findings, and protection from retaliation. [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/ai-safety-evaluators-anthropic-openai-models-security.html)
- **King Charles III presses OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and Nvidia leaders on AI safety** — At a gathering at Dumfries House in Scotland, the king told executives including Nvidia's Jensen Huang and DeepMind's Demis Hassabis that AI's "substance and pace" are "deeply concerning" and urged the industry to keep development in service of people and the planet. [Source](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/09/19/business/foreign-business/uks-king-charles-iii-meets-big-tech-leaders/2428343)
