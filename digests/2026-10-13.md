# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-13

Quiet day for new model launches and open-source releases — today's notable development is in AI industry dealmaking.

## Other News
- **Amazon reportedly in talks to acquire AI startup Decart for about $7 billion** — The Wall Street Journal reports Amazon is negotiating to buy Decart, whose software helps AI models run portably across Nvidia GPUs, Google TPUs, and Amazon's own Trainium chips; the startup also builds video/world models including Lucy and Oasis. Decart was valued at nearly $4 billion in May, and Bloomberg separately reported Anthropic had explored acquiring it for around $6 billion before dropping the effort. No deal has been finalized.
