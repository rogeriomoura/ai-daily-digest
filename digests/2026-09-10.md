# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-10

## Model Releases
- **DeepSeek ships V4.1 Flash with native multimodal support** — The update brings a new architecture, stronger capability, and faster inference to DeepSeek's efficient Flash line, with DeepSeek claiming it beats V4 Pro on internal tests while keeping V4-Flash pricing. It rolled out around September 10 Beijing time via a limited test endpoint. [Source](https://www.deepseek.com/en/news/deepseek-v4-1-flash/)

## Other News
- **NSA, CISA, and FBI accuse six Chinese AI firms of industrial-scale model distillation** — The joint advisory names DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, Alibaba, MiniMax, StepFun, and Z.AI, alleging they pulled billions of tokens from Claude, GPT, Gemini, and Grok to train their own models since late 2024. Rather than recommending outright blocking, the agencies advise US AI companies to quietly degrade responses for accounts flagged with high confidence as malicious distillers. [Source](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa26-251a)
- **Paul Christiano joins OpenAI's Foundation Board and Safety and Security Committee** — The RLHF co-inventor and Alignment Research Center founder, who led OpenAI's alignment team from 2017–2021, returns as a non-voting board observer working alongside committee chair Zico Kolter; he'll recuse himself from OpenAI-related matters and model evaluations given his other advisory roles. [Source](https://openai.com/index/paul-christiano-joins-openai-foundation-board/)
- **Anthropic launches an interactive Economic Scenario Explorer projecting AI's effect on US growth through 2030** — Version 1.0 models three scenarios ranging from modest (1.6% above no-AI GDP) to extreme ($44.4T GDP, 32.4% above baseline) but also extreme downside effects on wages and unemployment. Anthropic cautions the model omits policy responses, business cycles, and catastrophic-risk scenarios. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/institute/econ-scenarios)
- **AI sales-automation startup Clay raises $115M Series D at a $7.1B valuation** — The Wellington Management-led round more than doubles Clay's valuation from its Series C a year ago; the company says it serves over 17,000 customers, including 80% of the Forbes AI 50, and is launching a $1M fund to train "GTM engineers" for AI-driven revenue workflows. [Source](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/09/sales-automation-startup-clay-boosts-valuation-to-7-1b-in-115m-funding-round/)
