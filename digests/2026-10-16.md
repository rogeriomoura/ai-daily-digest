# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-16

A quiet day for new model releases and open-source projects — nothing freshly verified beyond what's already in recent digests. AI safety governance was the main story.

## Other News
- **Anthropic gives METR "employee-like" access to its systems, the deepest tier yet of outside AI evaluation** — METR, a nonprofit AI evaluator, now has six staff with company hardware, office space, and internal tool access comparable to Anthropic's own risk teams; Anthropic says it cannot block METR's publications, only require narrowly defined redactions (customer data, privileged legal material, certain model-architecture details), and METR can publicly say if it disagrees with a redaction. The move follows a Reuters report days earlier that Anthropic and OpenAI had sought tighter control over what independent evaluators could publish, which Anthropic disputed as not fully characterizing its position. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/an-update-on-independent-evaluators)
- **Anthropic opens a Singapore office and signs an AI safety MOU with the Singapore government** — The memorandum, signed with the Ministry of Digital Development and Information at Singapore International Cyber Week, covers AI safety testing standards, incident response, and responsible-adoption research; Anthropic says Singapore ranks second of 121 countries in per-capita Claude.ai usage and that over 1,500 GovTech engineers use Claude Code. No investment or headcount figures were disclosed. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/singapore-office-opening)
