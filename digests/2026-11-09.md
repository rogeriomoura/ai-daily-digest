# AI Daily Digest — 2026-11-09

No same-day model release surfaced today, but a few stories from late last week firmed up enough to report: Moonshot's Kimi K3.1 Pro open weights — flagged as "pending" in this digest on November 5 — actually went live, Cognition shipped a faster/cheaper coding model, and multimodal-inference startup fal disclosed a large combined raise.

## Open Source
- **Moonshot AI's Kimi K3.1 Pro open weights confirmed live** — Multiple outlets (TechNode, The Decoder, VKTR, Forklog) report Moonshot published full weights for Kimi K3.1 Pro on Hugging Face around November 4–5, following through on the release this digest had flagged as unconfirmed on November 5. It's a roughly 2.8-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model (~104B active) with a 1,048,576-token context window, released under a Kimi-specific license; Moonshot reportedly stripped cyber-related data from training before the open release, and several outlets call it the highest-scoring open-weight model currently available. Benchmark claims are Moonshot's own. [Source](https://technode.com/2026/11/05/moonshot-ai-releases-kimi-k3-1-pro-model-weights/)

## Other News
- **Cognition launches SWE-2 Flash, a faster/cheaper coding model for Devin** — Announced November 4 on Cognition's blog, SWE-2 Flash generates tokens roughly 3x faster than Cognition's larger SWE-2 model at about one-fifth the cost, and shipped in Devin Desktop and Devin CLI (free through November 30, per Cognition). It's built on Thinking Machines' open-weight "Inkling-Small" (276B total/12B active parameters vs. SWE-2's 2.8T/104B), but Cognition is keeping its own fine-tuned weights private. Reported benchmarks are mixed and company-sourced — slightly ahead of SWE-2 on FrontierCode 2.0 but behind it on Terminal-Bench 4.0 and DeepSWE 1.1. [Source](https://cognition.com/blog/swe-2-flash)
- **fal discloses $1.65B combined Series E/F, now valued at $16B** — Reported November 3–4 by SiliconAngle and Tech.eu, generative-media inference startup fal disclosed a $1.3B Series F led by Bessemer Venture Partners (with Nvidia, Sound Ventures, HOF Capital, and T. Rowe Price participating), plus an earlier, previously-unannounced ~$350M Series E led by Sequoia — a combined $1.65B that roughly doubles the company's valuation to $16B. The jump reflects surging demand for AI image/video inference capacity. [Source](https://tech.eu/2026/11/04/fal-doubles-valuation-to-16b-after-13b-series-f/)
