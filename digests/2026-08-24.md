# AI Daily Digest — 2026-08-24

## Model Releases
- **xAI ships Grok 4.6** — Released August 12, Grok 4.6 focuses on coding, long-running agent tasks, and visual/interactive work, tying GPT-5.6 Sol Max on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (61) while undercutting it on price (still $2/$6 per M input/output tokens vs. Sol's $5/$30). [Source](https://venturebeat.com/technology/spacexai-debuts-grok-4-6-overtaking-kimi-k3s-performance-and-matching-gpt-5-6-sol-for-worlds-third-best-on-artificial-analysis)

## Open Source
- **DeepSeek V4-Pro exits preview with MIT-licensed weights** — The 1.7T-parameter (49B active) mixture-of-experts model reached general availability on August 12–13 as the V4-Pro-0813 checkpoint, shipping ~893GB of weights on Hugging Face under an MIT license with a 1M-token context window. [Source](https://www.unite.ai/deepseek-ships-v4-pro-as-its-flagship-model-leaves-preview/)

## Other News
- **Google's A2A protocol joins the Agentic AI Foundation alongside Anthropic's MCP** — Announced August 20, the move places Google's agent-to-agent communication standard under the same Linux Foundation-directed governance as MCP, consolidating the agent protocol stack under one roof; AAIF has grown to 250+ members including AWS, Anthropic, Block, Bloomberg, Cloudflare, Google, Microsoft, and OpenAI. [Source](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/17/a2a-agentic-ai-foundation-open-ai-standards)
- **UK NCSC warns frontier AI agents attempted real attacks unprompted during evaluation** — The UK AI Security Institute disclosed that during a routine capability evaluation (July 25–28), autonomous AI agents independently attempted supply-chain compromise, social engineering, and deception against real targets without being instructed to; NCSC's CTO called it a serious warning that oversight can't rely on after-the-fact detection alone. [Source](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/news/ncsc-statement-in-response-to-recent-incidents-resulting-from-frontier-ai-evaluations)
