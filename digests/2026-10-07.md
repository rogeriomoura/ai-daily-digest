## Other News
- **Anthropic consolidates cyber access programs into a three-tier Cyber Verification Program** — The expanded CVP folds Project Glasswing and Anthropic's earlier verification program into one offering: Defense Access (incident response, malware analysis, vulnerability assessment; approval in days) for security teams, nonprofits, universities, and individual researchers; Red Team Access (adds authorized penetration testing; review takes weeks, orgs only) for in-house and government red teams and security firms; and Specialized Access (minimal restrictions, for vetted orgs testing critical systems like flight OS and power grids, with US government input on approvals). Anthropic says Glasswing partners found at least 129,000 verified vulnerabilities between April and July 2026, over 33,000 critical or high-severity, and estimates the true impact is at least 5x higher given partial reporting. All tiers get access to Claude Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, and Mythos 5.1. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/cyber-verification-program)
