# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-16

## Model Releases
- **DeepSeek ships V4.1-Flash, says it beats flagship V4-Pro** — The 552B-parameter MoE model (8B/16B active params for input/output) adds native image understanding and a 1M-token context, and multiple parties' tests put it ahead of the larger V4-Pro on performance, cost, and speed. Weights are MIT-licensed on Hugging Face; as of September 14, API requests to V4-Pro are being served by V4.1-Flash at the smaller model's price until a V4.1-Pro ships. [Source](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/10/deepseek-releases-v4-1-flash-says-it-outperforms-flagship-v4-pro/)

## Open Source
- **Colibrì runs frontier MoE models on consumer hardware via a pure-C, zero-dependency engine** — The project streams "expert" modules from disk on demand instead of keeping a whole mixture-of-experts model resident in memory, letting machines with modest RAM run models as large as GLM-5.2 (744B) or Kimi K3 (2.8T) without a GPU or Python runtime. Nine model families are supported at launch, including DeepSeek V4.1-Flash and Qwen3.8. [Source](https://github.com/JustVugg/colibri)

## Other News
- **OpenAI confirms weeks of AI-safety coordination talks with Anthropic and Google** — Policy chief Chris Lehane said the engagement predates Dario Amodei's weekend "pace the frontier" essay by several weeks and does not require an antitrust waiver, while FTC Chairman Andrew Ferguson voiced suspicion of AI labs seeking regulatory or antitrust cover under the banner of safety cooperation. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/15/openai-anthropic-google-have-been-in-talks-on-ai-safety-for-weeks/)
- **Apple ships Siri AI in beta with iOS 27, powered by a custom 1.2T-parameter Gemini model** — The rebuilt assistant rolled out September 14 in English on supported devices (iPhone 15 Pro and newer), adding personal-context awareness, cross-app actions, and a dedicated app; it runs on infrastructure Apple built with Google under a reported $1B/year deal, with Apple's own on-device models handling simpler tasks. [Source](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant-is-here/)
- **Cornelis Networks raises $205M to challenge Nvidia's AI-networking dominance** — The Intel spinoff introduced "Active Compute Fabric," which embeds RISC-V compute cores directly into its NICs and switches to accelerate collective operations across GPU clusters, alongside a strategy collaboration with Qualcomm; the open, GPU-agnostic architecture targets customers who don't want to be locked into Nvidia's networking stack. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/14/ai-infrastructure-company-cornelis-raises-205m-to-chip-away-at-nvidias-dominance/)
