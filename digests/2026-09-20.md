# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-20

## Open Source
- **trycua/cua pushes "computer-use 2.0" with cross-OS agent fleets** — The MIT-licensed project provides open-source drivers, cloud/local sandboxes for macOS, Windows, and Linux, and benchmarks (Cua Bench) for training and evaluating agents that operate full desktops via CLI, MCP, or SDKs, reflecting a broader trend toward agent infrastructure over single-agent demos. [Source](https://github.com/trycua/cua)

## Other News
- **Plugin4Shell: zero-click RCE disclosed in Claude Code, Codex, Copilot, and Gemini CLI** — Researchers at AIR found a plugin SHA-pinning bypass affecting all four major coding agents: a maliciously named branch can make a checkout resolve to attacker-controlled code while the agent still reports the expected commit hash as verified, exposing local source, cloud credentials, and secrets. Anthropic patched Claude Code (2.1.179) and OpenAI patched Codex (0.146.0); Copilot was unpatched at disclosure and Google said it will not fix the deprecated Gemini CLI. [Source](https://www.air.security/blog-posts/plugin4shell)
- **Anthropic and Accenture pledge $1B+ each to embed independent AI evaluators** — Accenture's Faculty unit will place evaluators inside Anthropic with access "comparable to an employee's," observing models during training and red-teaming, alignment-testing, and safeguard-testing them directly rather than auditing only finished releases. The partnership is non-exclusive; Anthropic says it's in talks with METR and other nonprofits on similar embedded-evaluation arrangements. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/accenture-embedded-evaluation)
- **Trump says he'll create an "AI Force" and name an AI czar** — Modeled on the Space Force, the initiative aims to accelerate US AI development while resisting new regulation; Trump gave no details on structure or leadership but said the government would "cherish it, help it, and watch over it" while pursuing bad actors under existing law. [Source](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/19/trump-ai-czar-space-force-safety)
- **Clinicians push back on medical AI's expansion beyond diagnostics** — The Financial Times reports doctors and nurses are skeptical of AI tools moving past established imaging/diagnostic uses into broader clinical workflows, citing thin peer-reviewed performance data; a Wolters Kluwer/Ipsos survey of 355 US clinicians found deskilling and hallucinations each flagged by 74% of respondents, with governance-policy awareness rising only from 21% to 27% year over year. [Source](https://www.techmeme.com/260920/p5)
