# AI Daily Digest — 2026-08-25

## Open Source
- **Prime Intellect open-sources Prime Agent, a self-improving coding harness** — Released under MIT license, Prime Agent pairs a "recursive language model" design with a persistent IPython kernel as its only tool, letting it program over its own history, spawn sub-agents, and apply small, evidence-backed edits to its own harness state via a `/refine` command. It scored 95.5% on ARC-AGI-3, edging past the reported 95.4% human-expert baseline, and has been one of GitHub's fastest-growing AI repos this week. [Source](https://github.com/PrimeIntellect-ai/prime-agent)
