## Other News
- **Oracle invokes "force majeure" on its $400B Stargate data center in New Mexico** — Oracle sent the notice to Blue Owl, developer of the Project Jupiter campus, citing delays in securing power (including a natural-gas pipeline pushed back nearly six months to February 2027) and legal challenges over water and air-quality permits. [Source](https://www.sanders.senate.gov/press-releases/news-sanders-casar-introduce-legislation-to-create-new-federal-agency-to-ban-artificial-superintelligence-pause-advanced-ai-development/)
