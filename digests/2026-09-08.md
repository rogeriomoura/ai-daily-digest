# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-08

## Open Source
- **MiniMax and NVIDIA ship Sol-H3, an inference stack faster than video playback** — Built on NVIDIA's Sana/Sol-Engine runtime, the stack generates 5 seconds of 1344×768 video with synced stereo audio from open-weight MiniMax-H3 (33B params) in 1.653 seconds on an 8×B300 system — up to 15.5x faster than the base model, via dynamic sparse attention, fused kernels, and parallel VAE decoding. It's a notable step toward real-time, interactive video generation rather than batch clip rendering. [Source](https://nvlabs.github.io/Sana/Sol-Engine/H3/)

## Other News
- **Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley push to get OpenAI and Anthropic investment-grade ratings the moment they IPO** — Per FT reporting, the banks are lobbying the big three credit agencies to rate both companies investment-grade at listing, opening access to the $11.7T corporate bond market for cheaper compute-buildout financing. It's a stretch by traditional metrics — OpenAI posted a $20.9B operating loss on $13.1B revenue in 2025, and neither company expects to break even before 2028-2030 — but mirrors the playbook SpaceX used after its IPO. Anthropic is reportedly still eyeing a late-September listing at a $2T valuation pitch. [Source](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2026:newsml_L4N4500AG:0-openai-anthropic-bankers-seek-investment-grade-credit-ratings-post-ipo-ft-says/)
- **California's Frontier AI Safety Act awaits Newsom's signature** — The bill cleared both legislative chambers in late August and now sits with Governor Newsom, who has until September 30 to sign or veto it; he vetoed a similar frontier-model bill in 2024 before signing the narrower SB 53 transparency law in 2025. The decision will shape how far the state goes in regulating catastrophic-risk frontier models ahead of expected federal preemption fights. [Source](https://cubbbix.com/blog/ai-regulation-september-2026-global-update)
