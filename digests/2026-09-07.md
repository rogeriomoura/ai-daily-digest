# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-07

## Open Source
- **RWKV-7 "Goose" scales to a 13.3B-parameter checkpoint under Apache 2.0** — The G1d release extends the attention-free, constant-memory recurrent architecture with notably stronger code and STEM performance, shipping alongside smaller 7.2B/2.9B/0.1B variants that share the same training recipe. It remains one of the few fully open, permissively licensed alternatives to transformer architectures for long-context, low-memory inference. [Source](https://github.com/BlinkDL/RWKV-LM)

## Other News
- **Nvidia confirms $12.9B deal to acquire Hugging Face** — The chipmaker's second-largest acquisition on record gives it control of the platform hosting roughly 3 million models used by over 18 million developers; Hugging Face reportedly approached Nvidia's Jensen Huang about a deal weeks in advance, after rejecting a $500M offer last year. The purchase, expected to close in H1 2027, extends Nvidia's push up the AI stack beyond hardware. [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/03/nvidia-agrees-to-buy-hugging-face-for-almost-13-billion-ai-expansion.html)
- **Google and HHMI Janelia publish the first complete connectome of a fruit fly's brain and nerve cord** — The map covers over 166,000 neurons and 125 million synapses, the largest brain map by neuron count to date, and is the first to extend from the brain into the ventral nerve cord that controls the body. AI-powered image reconstruction sped up connectome generation by more than 1,000x compared to earlier manual methods. [Source](https://research.google/blog/a-connectomics-milestone-mapping-the-complete-male-fruit-fly-brain/)
- **Gimlet Labs raises $300M Series B at a $3B valuation for multi-chip AI inference** — The Andreessen Horowitz-led round backs Gimlet's "multi-silicon" inference cloud, which splits AI workloads across GPUs, CPUs, and custom accelerators from Nvidia, AMD, Intel, Arm, Cerebras, and d-Matrix to cut latency and cost. The company says it has already secured billions in contracted revenue and is scaling toward hundreds of megawatts of managed capacity. [Source](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/04/gimlet-labs-nabs-300m-for-its-disaggregated-inference-platform/)
