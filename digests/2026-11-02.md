# AI Daily Digest — 2026-11-02

A quiet Sunday for new model drops, but three threads from the past few days are worth catching up on: a shared robots.txt signal for opting out of AI training now backed by multiple major labs, Meta's Muse agent expanding to Singapore, and OpenAI letting Pro users text their "Dots" agents directly.

## Other News
- **OpenAI, Google, and Microsoft back a shared robots.txt token for opting out of AI training** — Announced October 30, the three labs said they'll honor a single policy token (`__usage_ai-training`) that lets a site opt out of AI training across crawlers with one line, rather than listing each bot individually. The signal is defined in an IETF Internet-Draft authored by Anthropic's Kevin Kelley; reports differ on whether Anthropic itself has formally signed on, though one account says Anthropic is already implementing it while OpenAI targets "coming months" and Microsoft and Google are aiming for 2027. The token covers training only — it doesn't block crawling, search indexing, or AI answers quoting page content. [Source](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/openai-google-microsoft-anthropic-back-one-ai-opt-out-token)
- **Meta brings its personal AI agent Muse to Singapore** — Confirmed via Meta's own newsroom on October 28, Muse — Meta's "personal AI agent" that can shop, book travel, and fill out forms — launched in Singapore on iOS, Android, and the web, following its earlier US rollout. Launch partners include Shopee, Grab, Singapore Airlines, Agoda, Klook, and Lazada, with the agent designed to check in with users before sensitive actions like purchases. [Source](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/10/muse-is-now-available-in-singapore-the-worlds-first-personal-ai-agent-built-for-everyone/)
- **OpenAI lets US ChatGPT Pro users text their "Dots" agents via iMessage and RCS** — Rolled out October 28 as a limited beta, users can now message their always-on Dot agent directly from their phone's native texting app — iMessage on iPhone, RCS on Android — after verifying their number in ChatGPT. Context and plugin access carry over from the app, and the agent can text back proactively with updates. Pricing and wider regional availability (beyond the US) remain unclear. [Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/openai-dots-imessage-rcs-pro-users/)
