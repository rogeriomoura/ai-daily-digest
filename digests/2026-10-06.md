# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-06

## Model Releases
- **Reflection AI debuts Beam, a 501B-parameter open-weight model aimed at Chinese rivals** — The Brooklyn-based, Nvidia-backed startup's first frontier release is a text-only mixture-of-experts model (23B active parameters, pre-trained on 23.8T tokens, 1M-token context) built for reasoning, coding, and agentic tasks. Reflection claims Beam matches Z.ai's GLM-5.2 on advanced reasoning benchmarks while using 3-4x less inference compute than leading Western open models — a claim that hasn't been independently verified. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/reflection-debuts-beam-a-open-weight-ai-model-to-rival-chinese-models-at-lower-compute-cost/)

## Other News
- **OpenAI starts watermarking ChatGPT and Codex text in the EU** — To comply with the EU AI Act's Article 50 transparency rules, OpenAI is rolling out an invisible "textGrain" watermark that subtly adjusts word choices to leave a detectable statistical pattern, without changing visible output. Detection is unreliable once text is edited: in testing, replacing 25% of words with synonyms dropped detection from ~92% to ~17%. API developers worldwide can opt in now for select models; it's off by default. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/openai-will-start-watermarking-chatgpts-text-in-the-eu/)
