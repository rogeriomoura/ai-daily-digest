# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-14

## Other News
- **OpenAI shelves 2026 IPO plans, Altman cites AI safety concerns** — CEO Sam Altman told staff that going public this year would be an "ill-advised moment" given industry safety worries, walking back June's confidential draft IPO filing; OpenAI now targets 2027 instead. [Source](https://fortune.com/2026/09/12/sam-altman-openai-ipo-delay-ill-advised-moment-safety-concerns/)
- **Anthropic reportedly still targeting ~$2 trillion valuation for an October IPO** — Even as OpenAI pumps the brakes, bankers and investors are said to be pricing an Anthropic Nasdaq listing at up to $2T, based on projected 2028 revenue of $190–200B, with Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and Morgan Stanley leading an offering that could raise over $60B. [Source](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/ipo-watch/2026/anthropic-2-trillion-ipo-valuation-target-2026-08-25)
- **Microsoft's Nadella backs "pacing" AI development, to publish a Code of Conduct for its MAI models** — Responding to Dario Amodei's "We Must Pace the Frontier" essay, Nadella wrote that superintelligence work "has to be grounded" in staying helpful and human-controlled, and said Microsoft would open its first behavior guidelines for MAI models to public consultation. [Source](https://dataconomy.com/2026/09/14/satya-nadella-says-superintelligence-must-remain-human-controlled/)
- **California enacts Adam's Law (SB 1119), the country's toughest AI companion-chatbot child-safety rules** — Named for a teenager whose family says a chatbot contributed to his death, the law requires crisis protocols for suicidal ideation, parental controls, independent child-safety audits, and pre-release risk assessments for companion chatbots. [Source](https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/10/governor-newsom-signs-the-strongest-child-safety-chatbot-and-social-media-laws-in-the-nation/)
- **Universal Music Group and ElevenLabs sign a licensed AI music remix platform deal** — The multi-year, opt-in-by-artist agreement lets fans remix and reinterpret UMG tracks with AI, notable as a major label/AI deal reached without a preceding lawsuit. [Source](https://variety.com/2026/music/news/umg-elevenlabs-ai-powered-music-platform-licensing-1236857240/)
