# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-13

## Model Releases
- **Cognition ships SWE-2, a Kimi K3 post-trained coding model for Devin** — Post-trained from Moonshot AI's 2.8T-parameter Kimi K3, SWE-2 scores within one point of Claude Fable 5.1 on the FrontierCode 1.1 benchmark while costing about 64% less, and uses 58% fewer turns than its predecessor SWE-1.7. It has no open weights or standalone API, running only inside Devin (Desktop and CLI now, Web and Fusion rolling out). [Source](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/12/cognition-releases-swe-2-a-kimi-k3-post-trained-coding-model-that-matches-fable-5-1-on-frontiercode-at-64-lower-cost/)

## Other News
- **Anthropic's Amodei publishes essay urging labs to "slow the pace" of AI capability gains; Altman publicly agrees** — In a ~3,800-word essay, Dario Amodei warned that AI progress is outrunning researchers' ability to understand and control it and cautioned that swarms of rogue agents could threaten the internet within six months; he committed Anthropic to an embedded third-party evaluator team. OpenAI's Sam Altman quickly said his company agrees the industry should slow frontier-model advances and lean more into safety, marking a rare public alignment between the two labs' CEOs. [Source](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/12/anthropic-ai-amodei-pacing)
- **Anthropic's September threat intelligence report says newer Claude models can no longer be assumed safely below the bioweapons-assistance threshold** — The report catalogs Claude misuse across seven harm areas (cyber operations, influence campaigns, surveillance, scams, biological and conventional weapons development, and model distillation) identified and disrupted between December 2025 and August 2026. It's the first time a major AI lab has publicly said its models may meaningfully assist bioweapons development. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026)
- **Pentagon reportedly in talks to lend AI cloud startup Fluidstack $5B** — The loan, from the Defense Department's Office of Strategic Capital, would reportedly go toward manufacturing capacity for data-center components rather than expanding Fluidstack's footprint directly. Fluidstack already has data-center partnerships with Google and Anthropic; the deal is unfinalized and could still change. [Source](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/pentagon-in-talks-to-loan-fluidstack-5bn-report/)
