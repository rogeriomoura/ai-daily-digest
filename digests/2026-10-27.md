# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-27

No major new model drops today, but Anthropic took its first formal step toward going public, and two agent-tooling projects surged on GitHub Trending.

## Open Source
- **obra/superpowers, an agentic skills framework, keeps climbing GitHub Trending** — Built by Jesse Vincent with the Prime Radiant team and released under MIT, it bundles a full agentic development loop (brainstorming, planning, TDD, debugging, verification, subagent-driven work, git worktrees) as composable "skills" for Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, and Gemini CLI, plus the mechanism that lets an agent find and invoke them automatically. It gained roughly 600 stars today on top of an already large base. [Source](https://github.com/obra/superpowers)
- **OpenBot, a local-first multi-agent desktop workspace, gains traction** — Published by Synthetify Labs, OpenBot gives each AI "teammate" (running on top of Codex, Claude Code, Gemini, Grok, or local models via Ollama/LM Studio) its own persistent workspace, letting agents message each other, hand off tasks, and share files across macOS, Windows, and Linux. It's source-available under PolyForm Noncommercial rather than a standard OSS license, and added nearly 800 stars in a day. [Source](https://github.com/nightly-labs/openbot)

## Other News
- **Anthropic confidentially files a draft S-1 with the SEC, taking its first formal step toward an IPO** — Announced October 26, the filing doesn't set a share count or price, and Anthropic says any public offering still depends on SEC review and market conditions; coverage ties the move to its recent funding round valuing the company at roughly $965 billion. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news)
