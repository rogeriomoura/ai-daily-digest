# AI Daily Digest — 2026-08-27

## Model Releases
- **Mystery "OX Alpha" model tops coding benchmarks, free preview ending this week** — An anonymous "stealth" model appeared on OpenRouter/OpenCode on August 20 under the handle `stealth/ox-alpha`, offering a 1M-token context window and free access with generous rate limits. It topped DeepSWE (80%, ahead of Claude Fable 5's 65%) and placed second on Kingbench behind GLM 5.3; community fingerprinting of its tokenizer and behavior points (unconfirmed) to Zhipu's next-gen GLM line. [Source](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/ox-alpha-stealth-model-frontend)

## Other News
- **OpenAI's first custom chip, "Jalapeño," claims wins over Nvidia's Blackwell** — Co-developed with Broadcom and Celestica, the inference-only chip was benchmarked at Hot Chips 2026 with 1.5–1.9x more AI work per watt and up to 4.1x higher interactive-workload performance than commercial systems. SemiAnalysis flagged the comparison as somewhat unfair since Jalapeño uses newer HBM4 memory than the Blackwell units tested. [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/openai-jalapeno-ai-chip-nvidia.html)
- **Researchers crack encrypted reasoning traces across OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google APIs** — A paper found that opaque "thinking block" objects from reasoning APIs could be replayed into a different, weaker sibling model from the same provider, which would then decode and print the hidden reasoning verbatim — enabling distillation theft, private-data leakage from shared traces, and hidden prompt injection. The team decoded over 315,000 thinking blocks across ~6,700 public agent trajectories; Anthropic now advises stripping thinking blocks when switching models. [Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/openai-anthropic-google-api-flaw-let.html)
- **Amazon shuts down Mechanical Turk after 21 years** — The crowdsourced-labor marketplace that helped bootstrap machine-learning data labeling will close September 30, 2026, alongside SageMaker Ground Truth and Augmented AI, as Amazon exits human-data infrastructure entirely in favor of AI-era labeling startups like Scale AI and Mercor. [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/amazon-service-that-jeff-bezos-called-artificial-ai-is-shutting-down.html)
- **Unitree's post-IPO slump fuels humanoid-robotics bubble fears** — Shares of the Chinese humanoid robot maker have fallen roughly 45% from their debut-day peak (a 460% first-day pop), erasing about $30B in market value amid scrutiny of thin commercialization — 2025 humanoid revenue was ¥868M, with industrial use just 9% of it — and a 53% year-over-year drop in Q1 2026 adjusted profit. [Source](https://www.scmp.com/tech/tech-trends/article/3365459/unitrees-stock-slump-ipo-stokes-fears-bubble-chinese-humanoid-robotics)
