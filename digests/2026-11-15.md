## Model Releases
- **Google rolls Gemini 4 Carbon out of restricted preview to Ultra subscribers and developers** — Having limited Carbon to cybersecurity defenders through its Fairwind Program since the October 28 unveiling (covered here October 31), Google announced general availability on November 10: AI Ultra subscribers can now use it in the Gemini app, AI Mode in Search (in select countries), and the Antigravity coding environment, and paying developers/enterprises can call it via the Gemini API in Google AI Studio. Introductory API pricing is $2/$10 per million input/output tokens through November 30, rising to $4/$20 after; it isn't available on the free tier. [Source](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/)
