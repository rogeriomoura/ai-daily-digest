# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-05

## Other News
- **Anthropic says Claude produced the first fully machine-checked proof of Fermat's Last Theorem** — Working largely autonomously over 11 days on Prove2Me, an open collaborative math-formalization platform built at Columbia, a swarm of Claude agents generated 13 million lines of Lean 4 code and proved 30,300 theorems (29,500 used in the final proof), consuming about six billion output tokens from a research model comparable to Claude Fable 5.1. Mathematician Kevin Buzzard called it "extraordinary," saying it arrived far faster than experts expected and hints at practical automatic formalization of modern mathematics. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formalizing-fermats-last-theorem)
