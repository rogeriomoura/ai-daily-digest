# AI Daily Digest — 2026-10-01

## Model Releases
- **Google DeepMind previews an early Gemini 4 "much earlier" than year-end** — CTO Koray Kavukcuoglu said Gemini 4 has entered post-training and that Google intends to ship an early post-training version as soon as possible rather than wait for a polished final release, citing promising internal results; the model is already powering Google's Antigravity agentic dev environment internally. No firm release date has been set, though observers speculate it could land as soon as October. [Source](https://runtimewire.com/article/google-gemini-4-post-training-antigravity-release)
- **xAI ships Grok 4.7, its most capable coding and agentic model** — The new flagship adds multimodal (text + image) input, a 500K-token context window, and stronger self-verification for long-running debugging and refactoring tasks, while keeping Grok 4.6's pricing ($2/M input, $6/M output). xAI touts industry-leading jailbreak-resistance and biosafety benchmark results, though early reviews call it token-hungry with only incremental gains over 4.6. [Source](https://braindetox.kr/en/posts/grok_4_7_release_2026.html)

## Open Source
- **Alibaba's Qwen team open-sources Qwen-Image-2.1** — The 7B-parameter model unifies text-to-image generation and editing in a lightweight architecture, adding native transparent (RGBA) output, up to 10-reference-image editing with mask/annotation support, and native 2K resolution. Weights are on Hugging Face and ModelScope with day-zero ComfyUI/Diffusers support, though it ships under Qwen's research license rather than Apache 2.0, so commercial use needs a separate agreement. [Source](https://digg.com/ai/kzb6rkbq)

## Other News
- **Anthropic's draft IPO filing shows a $42B 2025 net loss as investors eye a $2T valuation** — Documents from Anthropic's confidential S-1 submission put 2025 net losses at nearly $42 billion against roughly $9-10 billion in revenue, even as run-rate revenue has since surged past $47 billion. Backers are reportedly modeling a $2 trillion-plus valuation for an expected October listing, which would make it the largest stock-market debut in history. [Source](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/anthropic-files-for-ipo-looking-to-beat-openai-to-the-punch/)
- **Trump administration launches America.gov, an AI chatbot for federal services** — The new site, unveiled this week alongside the broader AI.gov modernization push, aims to be a single access point across roughly 29,000 government websites, letting citizens renew passports and file taxes through a chatbot built on models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. The effort is led by a team with roots in the now-defunct DOGE initiative, and an internal sibling chatbot, GSAi, is already used by about 1,500 federal workers. [Source](https://blog.tmcnet.com/blog/rich-tehrani/ai/ai-gov-to-launch-with-federal-chatbot-and-api-tools.html)
