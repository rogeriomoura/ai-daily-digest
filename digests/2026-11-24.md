# AI Daily Digest — 2026-11-24

No new model releases or notable open-source drops surfaced today, but the Nvidia leadership saga covered in several recent digests took a dramatic turn: its board reversed course and reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO.

## Open Source
- **llama.cpp ships v1.0.0, its first stable release after 3.5 years, with semantic versioning** — Announced by the ggml-org team on November 13 and still not previously covered here, the release formalizes a semver contract for the libllama and libggml public C APIs (major-version bumps reserved for breaking changes), adds FP8 KV cache support, and hardens the built-in server; it's distributed via GitHub, Docker, Homebrew, conda-forge, Winget, and an Apple XCFramework. [Source](https://neurohive.io/en/news/llama-cpp-v1-stable-release-semver/)

## Other News
- **Nvidia's board reverses itself, reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO effective immediately** — Announced November 23 via Nvidia's own press release and widely confirmed (CBS News, Fox Business, ABC Australia, Variety), the board voted unanimously to reinstate Huang as president, CEO, and board director — 11 days after accepting his resignation and less than a week after Chair Harvey Jones told investors on the Q3 earnings call that the board "does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO." The board says it received input from senior U.S. government officials and is confident the reinstatement poses no national security risk; President Trump said he personally spoke with the board and "convinced" it to bring Huang back. Interim CEO Colette Kress returns to her CFO role; the board's independent investigation into the affair and foreign-intelligence-contact allegations that triggered Huang's resignation continues, and Senate Banking Chair Tim Scott says Huang will still testify publicly before his committee in December. Nvidia stock jumped roughly 31% on the news. [Source](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-ceo/)
- **Alphabet-backed Isomorphic Labs raises $5B Series C, one of the largest-ever drug-discovery AI rounds** — Announced November 17 (not previously covered here), the DeepMind spinout's round was co-led by Sequoia Capital and Greenoaks, with Andreessen Horowitz and continued participation from Alphabet; it's roughly 2.4x the valuation implied by its $2.1B Series B just six months earlier, bringing total disclosed funding to about $7.7B since 2025. The company says proceeds will fund global compute and R&D infrastructure as it pushes its first AI-designed drug candidates toward clinical trials; no valuation was disclosed, though Bloomberg had reported the company was seeking roughly $50B. [Source](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/isomorphic-labs-raises-5b-ai-drug-discovery)
