# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-09

## Open Source
- **Tencent open-sources Hy4 Preview, a 770B-parameter MoE flagship under Apache 2.0** — The model activates only 49B parameters per token across 77 expert layers, ships with a 1M-token context window and an FP8-quantized variant, and edged out GLM 5.3 and Kimi K3 on Tencent's internal engineering-task evaluation. Tencent says the model helped automate its own training and inference optimization, reportedly lifting its own serving throughput by nearly 32%. [Source](https://technode.com/2026/08/28/tencent-open-sources-hy4-preview-with-770b-parameters-and-a-1m-token-context/)

## Other News
- **Mistral raises a record €3B Series D at a €21B valuation, led by Samsung** — The round — Europe's largest-ever private tech raise — also drew the EU-backed Scaleup Europe Fund and PSG Equity, nearly doubling Mistral's valuation from its €11.7B Series C a year ago. Proceeds go toward frontier research, compute buildout, and international expansion as Mistral positions itself as Europe's "sovereign AI" champion. [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/08/mistral-raises-e3b-as-sovereign-ai-becomes-big-business/)
- **Qualcomm and Amazon strike a multi-generation deal on custom AI inference chips and optical networking** — The partnership covers custom inference silicon, 1.6T optical interconnects for AWS data centers, and use of AWS infrastructure in Qualcomm's chip-design flow. Amazon received warrants for up to 25M Qualcomm shares tied to as much as $60B in future purchases through 2036; Qualcomm's stock jumped 10% on the news.
