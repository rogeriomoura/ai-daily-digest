# AI Daily Digest — 2026-11-11

A noisier-than-usual news day to track: Anthropic's IPO roadshow is underway with investors and reporters floating a wide, shifting range of valuation figures, Meta quietly extended its "Muse" model family, and DeepSeek's open-sourced coding-agent prototype from last week continues to draw attention as a Claude Code rival.

## Model Releases
- **Meta releases Muse Ember 1.0, a lightweight model for agent sub-tasks** — Reported November 9 by multiple outlets (The New Stack, TechJack Solutions, OpenRouter, Artificial Analysis), Ember is the fourth model in Meta's "Muse" line (after Spark, Glimmer, and Wonder), positioned for the repetitive execution steps inside larger agent workflows rather than top-level reasoning. It has a 1M-token context window, image input, and tool calling, available via Meta's Model API and Muse Code at $0.10/$0.50 per million input/output tokens. Unlike the Apache-2.0 "Glimmer" and "Spark Open" models, Ember's weights are not open — it's proprietary. [Source](https://thenewstack.io/meta-muse-ember-launch/)

## Open Source
- **DeepSeek open-sources "Super-Agent," a proactive coding-agent prototype aimed at Claude Code** — First reported around October 31–November 1 (SCMP, Entrepreneur India, and others) but still generating coverage this week, Super-Agent is an MIT-licensed plugin for DeepSeek's agent scaffolding ("DeepSeek Harness") that can monitor a GitHub repo and, optionally, Lark/Feishu group chats, and proactively suggest fixes without being prompted. DeepSeek frames it as a research prototype rather than a finished product; it reportedly passed 4,000 GitHub stars within a day of release. No independent benchmarks against Claude Code or Codex have surfaced yet. [Source](https://india.entrepreneur.com/business-news/deepseek-releases-open-source-coding-agent-to-rival-claude-code)

## Other News
- **Anthropic's IPO roadshow is underway, but the reported valuation keeps moving** — Anthropic set a $70–$78/share price range on Nasdaq (ticker "ANT") earlier this week, implying up to ~$64B raised, and multiple outlets report the roadshow has now launched with strong stated investor interest. However, reporting is inconsistent and shifting: some outlets (CNBC, RTE, Yahoo) cite bankers discussing valuations as high as $1.4–1.5T or more, while others (Bloomberg, via secondary reports) say institutional buyers are pushing back toward $1.2–1.3T after last week's broader AI-stock selloff — down from a ~$2T figure floated a month ago. A listing before Thanksgiving, with pricing expected around the week of November 16, remains the widely reported target, but no final price or share count has been confirmed as of this writing. Treat specific valuation numbers as provisional. [Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/anthropic-ipo-could-top-1-094802740.html)
- **DOJ reportedly scrutinizing Nvidia's $12.93B Hugging Face acquisition** — Reported October 28 by Reuters (via multiple outlets including Let's Data Science and MLex), the Justice Department has been asking whether Nvidia's pending purchase of Hugging Face — announced in September and expected to close in H1 2027 — would give Nvidia outsized control over a key AI model-distribution platform, as part of a broader, ongoing antitrust review of Nvidia that also touches its SchedMD, Run:ai, and Groq deals. Sourcing is anonymous; DOJ has not publicly confirmed an investigation, and officials caution a probe doesn't imply wrongdoing. [Source](https://letsdatascience.com/news/doj-reportedly-opens-inquiry-into-nvidias-hugging-face-acqui-b6f36063)
