# AI Daily Digest — 2026-08-29

## Model Releases
- **Alibaba previews Qwen4 architecture with Qwen3.8-Flash-Next** — Alibaba's Qwen team open-sourced Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, a 125B-parameter multimodal MoE with only 6B active parameters, giving developers an early look at the architecture that will underpin the upcoming Qwen4 family. It performs just below Alibaba's flagship Qwen3.8-Max while costing roughly one-twelfth as much per token, with open weights on Hugging Face and ModelScope. [Source](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/26/alibabas-qwen-team-releases-qwen3-8-flash-next-a-125b-multimodal-moe-with-6b-active-parameters-previewing-the-qwen4-architecture/)

## Other News
- **Nvidia reportedly agrees to buy Hugging Face for ~$12.9B** — Reports diverge on whether a deal is fully signed or still under discussion at a $13B+ valuation, but if completed it would be Nvidia's largest acquisition ever, putting the leading open-weight model/dataset hub under a chipmaker's ownership and giving Nvidia early visibility into open models, agent frameworks, and robotics software. [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/27/nvidia-hugging-face-acquisition.html)
- **Salesforce and Anthropic launch "Claudeforce"** — The expanded partnership embeds Salesforce's CRM data and workflows into Claude via a new "Salesforce in Claude" plugin for Claude Cowork, shipping 37 prebuilt sales skills (meeting prep, deal-health reviews, pipeline analysis) that let sellers act on live CRM data without opening Salesforce. It's live for pilot customers now, with an open beta planned for September. [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/salesforce-anthropic-partnership-claudeforce.html)
