# AI Daily Digest — 2026-11-03

Another quiet stretch for new model and open-source releases, but the AI infrastructure financing story keeps escalating: Meta unveiled a roughly $95B equity raise to fund its AI buildout, with Nvidia, AMD, and Broadcom all expressing interest in buying stakes.

## Other News
- **Meta unveils plan to raise up to $95B in equity for AI infrastructure** — Announced October 26, the plan combines roughly $35B in public offerings of common and convertible preferred stock with a separate at-the-market program of up to $60B, the latter contingent on new non-voting Class C shares beginning to trade (expected Q1 2027). Meta said Nvidia, AMD, and Broadcom have expressed interest in buying up to $5B of Class A common stock combined, though it stressed those expressions aren't binding purchase commitments; one report separately describes a $3B Nvidia investment tied to an expanded AI partnership. The raise follows Meta's Q3 earnings, after which the company signaled still-larger AI capital spending for the year ahead.
