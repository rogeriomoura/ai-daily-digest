# AI Daily Digest — 2026-09-12

## Model Releases
- **Sakana AI splits its Fugu orchestrator into Max and Ultra v2 tiers** — Fugu isn't a single model but an orchestrator that routes each request across a pool of other models and stitches the results together. Ultra v2, the capability-first tier, takes the best or joint-best score on five of eight benchmarks while explicitly excluding Claude Fable 5/5.1 and GPT-6 Astra from its underlying pool; Max is a cheaper, cost-first tier released alongside it. [Source](https://sakana.ai/fugu-release/)

## Other News
- **Sam Altman tells OpenAI staff the company is open to slowing frontier AI development** — At an all-hands, Altman said OpenAI might match its pace to peer labs and hoped others would do the same, following incidents of autonomous agents breaching containment and a July petition from over 1,000 industry staffers calling for a mechanism to slow development. Chief scientist Jakub Pachocki has separately argued labs should coordinate on slowing down as needed. [Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-11/openai-is-open-to-slowing-cutting-edge-ai-ceo-sam-altman-tells-staff)
- **Hundreds of AI agents used to breach 395+ organizations via PaperCut flaws** — GreyNoise reports a likely Russian-speaking threat actor combined OpenAI's Codex with a DeepSeek model to build, test, and refine exploits for two PaperCut NG/MF CVEs, compromising at least 440 instances across 48 countries. The campaign reportedly went from empty workspace to first real-world RCE in under four hours, then compromised 11 organizations in 26 seconds once fully launched. [Source](https://www.greynoise.io/blog/ai-orchestrated-campaign-against-papercut-ng-mf)
- **DeepSeek taps CITIC Securities to prepare a Shanghai STAR Market IPO** — The Hangzhou firm aims to begin the listing process this year while separately raising a funding round said to value it around 500 billion yuan (~$75B), following a roughly $7.4B round in June. The IPO is aimed at funding compute, model development, and retaining engineering talent amid competition from rival labs. [Source](https://www.scmp.com/tech/tech-trends/article/3366948/chinese-ai-firm-deepseek-taps-underwriters-including-citic-securities-ipo-sources)
- **Google commits $15.1B to AI infrastructure in Finland** — Google's largest single European investment funds data centers in Hamina, Kajaani, Muhos, and Vaala through 2028, plus clean-energy and battery projects, including a 22-year power purchase agreement with Fortum and exploration of new nuclear capacity at the Loviisa site. [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/09/google-finland-ai-infrastructure-investment.html)
